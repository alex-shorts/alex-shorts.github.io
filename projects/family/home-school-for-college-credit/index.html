A light load (~15 hours) barely moves the calendar — still about 7–8 years. Fr / So / Jr / Sr is rough standing after hours already earned, not a locked sequence. Heavy is only in some circumstances.

Planning defaults: light 15 · medium 30 · heavy 60 semester hours. 30 hours ≈ one full-time year. Heavy matches TJC’s note that a student can earn up to 60 college hours, or an associate, by graduation.
