He raises the house

Temple. Church. Living stones. His presence here.

Zechariah 6:12–13 · 2 Samuel 7:13

The Branch shall build the temple of the Lord and sit and rule on his throne. He shall build a house for my name, and I will establish the throne of his kingdom forever.

John 2:19, 21 · Matthew 16:18

Destroy this temple, and in three days I will raise it up — the temple of his body. On this rock I will build my church, and the gates of hell shall not prevail against it.

Ephesians 2:20–22 · 1 Peter 2:5

Christ Jesus himself being the cornerstone… a dwelling place for God by the Spirit. You yourselves like living stones are being built up as a spiritual house.

1 Corinthians 3:9, 16 · Haggai 2:9 · Hebrews 3:6

You are God’s building, God’s temple. The latter glory of this house shall be greater than the former. We are his house.

That he may command his children and his household after him to keep the way of the Lord by doing righteousness and justice.

Psalm 127:1, 3–5

Unless the Lord builds the house… Children are a heritage from the Lord. Like arrows in the hand of a warrior… He shall not be put to shame when he speaks with his enemies in the gate.

Ruth 4:11 · Proverbs 24:27
