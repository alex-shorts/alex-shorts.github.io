The house that fills the earth

The temple is not one building in Jerusalem. It is one of the Bible’s master fractals: the same three rooms, at every scale — and each house has a season. The first house extends the garden from the epicenter. After the fall you come back toward the ark. After Jesus the pattern reestablishes: Light of the world to the nations, until the city needs no sun.

Where this sits

The House on campus is a first drawing: nation, city, court, temple, holy of holies. This report is the typology underneath that drawing. It reads the live plan as one instance of a pattern the whole canon repeats.

The claim

Heaven and earth were meant to overlap. A temple is any place God makes that overlap — then sends the overlap outward until there is no outside left. BibleProject’s own line: the whole biblical drama can be told as a story about God’s temple. They name it a macro temple and a miniature cosmos, a micro-Eden. This report keeps “fractal” as our word for the same nested three rooms at every scale.

How to read the nests

Do not flatten them into one ladder of rungs. Space, time, and people are the same house seen three ways. Sabbath is not a seventh court of Solomon. The lips are not a tiny Zion. They are the same grammar — inner, house, court, shine — at the size you are actually living.

1. The three rooms — one grammar

BibleProject asks you to set Genesis 1 beside 1 Kings 6: creation finished in seven days, Solomon’s house finished in seven years; sky / land / sea beside holy of holies / holy place / court. The cosmos is a macro-temple. The temple is a miniature cosmos. Image-bearers stand in it the way a statue stood in a pagan shrine — except they walk.

Inner Holy of holies. Rest. Glory. The ark. The tree in the midst. The Spirit in the man. The Lamb on the throne. East is away from here; you walk west toward presence.
House Holy place. Lampstand as tree. Bread. Incense. Priests who work and keep (abad and shamar — Genesis 2:15, Numbers 3:7–8). The rooms of a household. The soul. The church as living stones around the cornerstone.
Court The pavement that faces the land. Nations this far. The body. The city around the house. The waters Noah’s ark rides. The world the garden was meant to fill.
Not three gods, not three climbs

The rooms are one house. The House page was right to refuse a second ladder. Build is not another ascent of rungs. It is the same presence, nested, then spreading. Covering’s grammar still holds: you come under before you become a roof. The inner room is not a prize at the top of the mountain. It is the source that fills the courts.

BibleProject Classroom — the same three, named twice

Mackie’s Heaven and Earth notes (session 24) line the rooms up: holy of holies / heaven / midst of the garden · holy place / the land / the garden in Eden · courtyard (bronze sea) / the waters / the land outside. Genesis 1 is not called “temple” in the Hebrew; they argue from literary design (seven-day dedication, image as statue). That caution stays.

2. The seasons — a cycle, then a vector

If you only draw nested boxes, you have a museum. If you only draw shine-out, you skip the long age when the way was in. Each house has a place in time and a purpose. The rooms stay. The motion turns.

Garden The first temple extends the garden from the epicenter. Fill the earth. The river leaves Eden. Dominion is a sending, not a visit. Out
After the fall The east is a sword. The goal reverses: come back toward the ark of the covenant. Cherubim that blocked the garden now sit on the mercy seat. High priest, once a year. In
After Jesus The veil is torn. The pattern reestablishes. He is the Light of the world; the Spirit empowers a people to carry that light to the nations — until the city needs no sun. Out

Inside a sending season the vector is still a fountain:

Presence The glory fills the inner room. In
House Priests carry it through the rooms. Through
Court The nations may come this far — then further. Out
Earth The knowledge of the glory covers as the waters cover the sea. Fill
Genesis 1:28 · Genesis 3:24 · Hebrews 9:7 · Matthew 27:51 · John 8:12 · Acts 1:8 · Revelation 21:23

Holy, holy, holy — the whole earth is full of his glory. That hymn is the first house’s purpose. After the fall it becomes a pilgrimage: draw near. After the curtain tears it becomes a commission again: you will receive power, you will be witnesses. Ezekiel’s river, Isaiah’s mountain, and Pentecost are the sending grammar restored. Failure is always the same sin: keep the light, eat the tree for yourself, shut the court, refuse the nations, build a house while refusing the Builder.

Foreigner welcome 1 Kings 8:41–43, Isaiah 56, Ephesians 2:14. The court was never meant to stay empty of nations.
Cube / inner 1 Kings 6:20 beside Revelation 21:16. When the world is the inner room, the fractal completes.

5. The temples of Scripture

Not every sacred space is called “temple.” The authors still build them with the same stones: east, cherubim, tree or lamp, water going out, a shut inner room, a people who work and keep, a glory that either fills or departs.

Garden of Eden

BibleProject: all creation is God’s temple; in the middle of that temple God plants another — a garden. Eastward entrance. Mountain (Ezekiel 28:13–14). Rivers that split to the four corners (Genesis 2:10–14) — the first shine-out. Cherubim at the gate after the exile (Genesis 3:24), later embroidered on the veil. Adam and Eve as royal priests: abad and shamar, the same pair used of Levites guarding the tent (Numbers 3:7–8). The tree of life becomes the lampstand (Exodus 25:31–40). The tree of knowing sits like Torah in the ark — wisdom and life that must not be seized.

Library: purpose-of-blessings board already isolates Genesis 2:15. Exile theme on campus is humanity’s exile from this inner room.

Patriarch tents — mini Edens

BibleProject names Abraham, Isaac, and Jacob’s altar-and-tree camps as informal temples: tent, altar, tree, mountain. Bethel is “house of God” (Genesis 28:16–19). These are not Solomon’s courts. They are small overlaps on the way to a people — presence meeting a man so blessing can reach families of the earth (Genesis 12:3).

Noah’s ark

A three-deck house that rides the waters of un-creation. One door. The LORD shuts it (Genesis 7:16) — covering’s grammar: you enter, you remain. Eight souls, a remnant, then “be fruitful and fill the earth” again (Genesis 9:1). The ark is not a shrine with an altar court. It is a mobile holy place whose purpose is the same vector: presence preserving a people so the garden can start over. 1 Peter 3:20–21 reads baptism through that door.

Library: covering report already names the ark, Passover house, Rahab, and the city of refuge as doors. Keep that list; add the three decks as the three rooms in wood.

Sinai and the tabernacle

The mountain is already zoned: summit, mid-slope, camp — holy of holies, holy place, court. Then Exodus 25:9, 40: make everything after the pattern shown on the mountain. Heaven’s house copied in goat hair and gold. When it is finished, the cloud covers and Moses cannot enter (Exodus 40:34–35) — the same filling Solomon will see, and the same filling Acts 2 will relocate onto people. Israel is named a kingdom of priests, a holy nation (Exodus 19:6): the whole camp is a court around a house around a fire, aimed at the nations.

Library: psalm-65-67-84-temple board quotes Exodus 40 in full. Understanding-Philip board: the temple’s purpose was to draw near, not merely to diagnose sin; in the new creation the world itself is the house.

Solomon’s temple — and its ruin

Seven years (1 Kings 6:38). Garden carved into cedar. East. Inner oracle. Glory fills so the priests cannot stand (1 Kings 8:10–11; 2 Chronicles 7:1–3). The charge is the vector: this house is for the foreigner who hears of the Name (1 Kings 8:41–43). Failure is Solomon’s own: other gods, other courts. Then Ezekiel watches the glory leave eastward (Ezekiel 10–11). The House page’s court plan is this building remembered — nation, city, court, house, stone — after the glory has a new address.

Ezekiel’s river and the second house

A future house on a very high mountain, facing east (Ezekiel 40). Water from under the threshold, to the ankles, knees, loins, a river to swim in, trees on both banks, the sea healed (Ezekiel 47). That is Eden’s river and Revelation 22’s river in one vision. Haggai 2:9: the latter glory greater than the former — not because Herod gilds more gold, but because the Presence will not stay behind a veil.

Library: waters board and establishing-the-kingdom board already collect Isaiah’s desert rivers, Ezekiel 47, John 7, Revelation 22.

Jesus — the temple they could kill

“Destroy this temple, and in three days I will raise it up… he spoke of the temple of his body” (John 2:19–21). The Word tabernacled among us (John 1:14). The veil tears (Matthew 27:51): the inner room opens, not so tourists can stroll in, but so the glory can get out. He is priest, sacrifice, and house. BibleProject: Pentecost is the dedication of new human temples beside Solomon’s fire on stone.

Print sheet already joins Zechariah 6:12–13, John 2, Matthew 16:18.

Marriage — two, one body, one house

“The two shall become one flesh. This mystery is profound, and I am saying that it refers to Christ and the church” (Ephesians 5:31–32). A marriage is a small temple: an inner union, a household of rooms, a gate that faces the city (Proverbs 31:23). Fruitfulness is the shine-out — children as arrows, hospitality, a name in the gates. Song of Songs in the library already sets Ephesians 5 beside Revelation 21: the bride-city coming down, dwelling of God with man.

Wisdom’s house belongs here too: “Wisdom has built her house; she has hewn her seven pillars” (Proverbs 9:1). Seven is temple-dedication time. Folly tears the same house down (Proverbs 14:1). That is already a House-page verse.

The human body — body, soul, spirit

“Your body is a temple of the Holy Spirit within you” (1 Corinthians 6:19). “May your whole spirit and soul and body be kept blameless” (1 Thessalonians 5:23). The three rooms in a person: spirit as inner room (the Spirit bears witness with our spirit), soul as holy place (life, mind, the rooms filled — Proverbs 24:3–4), body as court (what the street can see, what can be presented as a living sacrifice — Romans 12:1). Defile the court and you have not left the inner room untouched. The library’s flesh-vs-spirit and lips-speech boards already park 1 Corinthians 3:16–17 and 6:19 here: you people are God’s temple; if anyone destroys it, God will destroy him.

The church — many members, one house

Christ the cornerstone; apostles and prophets the foundation; living stones; the whole structure growing into a holy temple; a dwelling place for God by the Spirit (Ephesians 2:19–22; 1 Peter 2:4–6; 1 Corinthians 3:9–17). Israel among the nations was the first “kingdom of priests.” The church is that vocation after the veil: not a replacement of Israel’s story but its Messiah-shaped opening to the Gentiles — the court, at last, without a wall of hostility (Ephesians 2:14). Many members, one body (1 Corinthians 12) is the same fractal as many stones, one house.

Israel, the nations, the world

Abraham: in you all families of the earth (Genesis 12:3). Isaiah 2: the mountain of the house lifted up, nations streaming in. Isaiah 49:6: too light a thing to raise up the tribes — I will make you a light to the nations. The House page already sits Daniel 2 here: the stone becomes a mountain and fills the whole earth. That is the fractal at civilizational scale: inner remnant, house of Jacob, court of the empires, then the earth as the Lord’s (Psalm 24:1) — the psalm that also lifts the gates.

Heavenly temple — the pattern

The psalm-65-67-84 board already writes this in so many words: the earthly house is a copy; Jesus is priest of the heavenly temple not made with hands, after Melchizedek, not Levi. Hebrews 8–10 is the board’s spine beside Exodus 40. The lips board then lists the forms in order: heavenly (the template) → tabernacle (portable meeting) → temple (heartbeat of Israel) → Jesus (“strike me down”) → church → the individual. That is our fractal list, already in the dump, waiting to be drawn.

Psalm 11:4 on the psalm-46-49 board: the LORD is in his holy temple; his throne is in heaven.

Sabbath — a temple that is a day

BibleProject calls it a cathedral in time. The Rest board already has the load-bearing texts: creation rest, the sojourner inside your gates (Exodus 20), Isaiah 58 delight, and Isaiah 56 — foreigners kept from saying “the LORD will separate me,” brought to the holy mountain, made joyful in the house of prayer for all peoples. Sabbath is the inner room of the week. Yom Kippur is the inner room of the year. The shine is the same: the court of time includes the Gentile.

Advent — temple versus father’s house

The Advent board sets Zechariah in the temple at the hour of incense, the people praying in the court, against Mary in a father’s house. Priestly line and Davidic line. Location is the point. “Veiled in flesh the Godhead see” is the hymn on that board: incarnation as tabernacle. The smallest house (a womb, a Nazareth room) is already the overlap.

Covering-doors — Passover, Rahab, refuge

The covering report already names them as doors into a house. They are temples of rescue: inner (the blood, the cord, the altar), house (stay in, remain, flee here), court (Egypt, Jericho, the land of blood-guilt). Jonah looks toward the holy temple from inside a shut fish — same grammar from the waters board. You enter; you remain; you come out to fill a city (Nineveh) with a word.

Heart, face, lips — the smallest gate

The lips board maps it: heart is the portal between body and spirit; face is the portal between people; veil is a portal blocker. 2 Corinthians 3: the veil lies on the heart until one turns to the Lord; unveiled faces behold glory and are changed. Proverbs 4:23: guard the heart, the springs. The mouth is the east gate of a personal temple. Purify the lips to enter; then speech is the river out. The Speech reports already work the fruit of that gate without naming it a court.

Psalm 1 — the planted man

A tree by streams, fruit in season, leaf that does not wither: a micro-Eden of one life. The wicked are chaff — outside. Psalm 84 on the temple board: highways to Zion in the heart; Baca made springs. The covering/Build spring (Isaiah 58) is the same water under a man before he is a mason.

False houses — Folly and Dagon

Wisdom calls from the heights; Folly sits in the same place and says the same words. Her house leads to Sheol (Proverbs 9). The government board parks the ark in Dagon’s temple: a captured glory that will not be housed; the idol falls, head and hands cut off on the threshold. Ezekiel’s departed glory is the same death in Solomon’s rooms. A perfect nested drawing with no presence is a tomb.

Cosmic close — no temple, because the Lamb is the temple

Revelation 21–22: new heaven and new earth, holy city as bride, dwelling of God with man, no temple in it — the Lord God and the Lamb are its temple. The city’s cube matches the holy of holies (1 Kings 6:20; Revelation 21:16): the whole world has become the inner room. River from the throne. Tree of life for the healing of the nations. Night is gone; the glory is the lamp. The pattern completes when the outer court is gone because there is no outside. Understanding-Philip’s note is exact: not that the temple was abolished as a bad idea; its purpose was nearness, and in the new creation the creation itself is that nearness.

8. How to show these fractals without clutter

The live House should stay one plan. This report is the legend behind the plan. If the campus drawing tries to label Eden, ark, marriage, and body at once, it will lie. Do this instead:

  • One diagram, many names. Keep nation / city / court / temple / holy of holies as the default. A scale row (as above) relabels the three rooms. Do not add a second nested system.
  • Always name the season. The same five courts mean garden-out, ark-in, or Christ-out depending on the house’s place in time. East at the bottom is the way in after the fall, and the way the light goes out after Jesus. The House page now leads with that cycle.
  • Three rooms, not seven courts. Altar court and holy place as separate click-targets fought the fractal. Inner, house, court is the grammar. City and nation are the land the court faces.
  • Pair this report to the print sheet. Front: the plan. Back: the verse well. This HTML: why the plan is allowed to mean a body, a marriage, a cosmos.
  • Next campus move, if any: done on The House — a scale row that does not change the geometry, only the names and the legend. Imagery first; method later. Still true.
The measure

Does the inner room shine, or have we locked it? A house, a body, a church, a nation can be a perfect nested drawing and still be a tomb. The fractal is not complete until the court is full of nations and the earth is full of glory.
