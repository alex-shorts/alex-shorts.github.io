Power / Fruit

The tongue bears fruit — life or death in its harvest.

Series overview

Where this sits

Bridge column between domain and path. Here speech is not only style or content; it produces outcomes — life, death, healing, or ruin.

Path note

Dual-path column: fruit of speech can feed many (10:21) or fill a person with death’s harvest (18:21).

Connections

Theme and typology links warranted by the matrix and the exploratory ontology — not every possible overlap, only the load-bearing ones.
