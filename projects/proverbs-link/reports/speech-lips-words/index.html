The speech typology

Speech is one domain with an inner order. Miro’s four columns are not four random piles — they move from restraint, to wise content, to fruit/power, and finally to the opposing wicked type.

1
Listen, Quiet & Control

Form restraint — hear before you speak.

2
Wise Words

Fill the mouth with knowledge worth teaching.

3
Power / Fruit

Watch the harvest — speech produces life or death.

4
Wicked / Evil

Name the opposing type — lips set on trouble.

How speech scales by life-stage

Same domain, widening responsibility — from the exploratory ontology’s child → king ladder.

ChildListen; receive teaching; hold the tongue
Youth / manGuard mouth; many words → sin; personal restraint
HouseholdSoft answer; cover offense; peace in the house
RulerCounsel, persuasion, gentle tongue under power
KingDecree; love righteous lips; speech at maximum scope
Why this matters for the reports

Read Listen as formation for every later rung. Read Power/Fruit as the bridge to Way of Life and Way of Death. Read Wicked/Evil as the death-path mirror — not a disconnected “bad words” list.
