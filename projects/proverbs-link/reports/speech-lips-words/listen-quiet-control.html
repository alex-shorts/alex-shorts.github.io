Listen, Quiet & Control

Guard the mouth. Still the rush. Make room to hear.

9 Proverbs 3 related Speech typology
Series overview

Where this sits

First movement of speech formation: restraint before expression. This column trains the person who will later speak wisely, counsel others, or rule with a soft tongue.

Path note

Primarily formative for the Way of Life — speech that does not rush into ruin.

Connections

Theme and typology links warranted by the matrix and the exploratory ontology — not every possible overlap, only the load-bearing ones.

↔
Anger

Soft answers and guarded lips keep fury from becoming speech-war (15:1; 25:15).

↔
Formation / Discipline

Listening and controlled speech are practiced before they become public counsel.

↔
Life-stage

Child → youth: listen and hold the tongue; later stages widen the same habit into counsel and decree.
