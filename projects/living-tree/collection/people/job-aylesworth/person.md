## Summary

**Probable** father of Otho Nial Aylesworth (**C250**). Homer Aylsworth **1887 pp. 62–63**: **Job (3)**—Philip (2), Arthur (1)—married **Sarah Clark**; entered Canada **1788**; settled Ernesttown near Bath; he, his wife, and three children died in the **1803** spotted-fever outbreak. Child list opens with **Otho Nial (4)** m. Charlotte Bowerman **1805**. Lineage on p. **89** restates Otho as son of Job (3). Soft-ingest only — `verified: false`. Birth **~1722** from aylesworth.net compiled — keep soft. Second wife **Sarah Aylsworth** (m. **26 Mar 1765**) is **not** soft-ingested as Otho’s mother (p. **90**). Parents soft-ingested as **Philip × Rachel Greene** (**C270**; 1887 p. **43**).
