# Obituary — Richard Dorland Shorts

**Source:** Ventura County Star (CA), 28 Oct 2009 — full text on [Find A Grave 65483876](https://www.findagrave.com/memorial/65483876/richard-dorland-shorts) (contributor S. McCoskey, May 2021)  
**Captured:** 2026-08-06 (research summary; full newspaper text on FAG)

---

Richard Dorland Shorts was born in Rochester, New York, July 29, 1922, the first son of Earl Stanley and Annabelle Lucretia Shorts. The family moved to Southern California in 1929. He graduated from South Gate High School in 1941.

He served in the U.S. Army Air Corps in World War II (Operations Specialist; Panama). After being discharged in 1945, he later worked as a fireman (Arcadia → Huntington Park; later Burbank Captain). His headstone reads CPL U.S. ARMY AIR FORCES WORLD WAR II.

He married Gloria Martha Gatz on October 22, 1950. They were married 59 years and had three children: Carla, Bill, and Gigi.

Richard passed away on October 23, 2009. He is buried at Conejo Mountain Memorial Park, Camarillo, Ventura County, California, with Gloria.
