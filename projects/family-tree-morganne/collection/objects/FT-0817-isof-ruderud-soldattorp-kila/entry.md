## Supports

- The place-name **Ruderud** is registered as a **soldattorp** (`st`) under hemman **Rud**, **Kila socken**, **Näs härad**, **Värmlands län**.
- A **gärde** (field) of the same name is also registered at Rud in that parish.
- Falck-Kjällquist 1973 p. 85 is the cited etymology for the field name (book not retrieved).

Does **not** give coordinates, a modern fastighet, or a photograph of the building. Does **not** name Carl Johan or any soldier. The map-sheet note on the 1939 card is **not** härad sheet J112-62-4 (that sheet is Kil/Fryken, wrong parish).
