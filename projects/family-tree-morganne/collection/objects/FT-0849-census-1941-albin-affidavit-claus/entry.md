## Catalog note

Three-page family packet used in 1941 (Immigration and Naturalization Service, San Diego, file 16542/D-44) plus a later notebook page.

Covering email: the 26 Sep 1941 paper shows parents and siblings of Claus; 30 Jul 1941 says eight children; 26 Sep lists seven; sender unsure which is correct. A later same-day note chose seven. Honest resolution: Albin counted **eight births**; the 1900 extract lists **seven living children** in the house. Catalog already has **Alma Eugenia Rud** (1885–1888, Kila) — the child who never emigrated.

### photos/02.jpg — Albin Evald Rudd affidavit, Corona, 30 Jul 1941

Typed statement, signature **Albin Evald Rudd**, witnessed by **W. E. Root**, Post Master, Arlington, Calif.; purple Arlington postmark **AUG 16 1941**.

> I, Albin Evald Rudd, 3174 Date Street, Riverside, California, do hereby swear to the following:
> I was born July 11, 1887 in Vermland, Sweden
> My father's name -- Carl Johan Rud (deceased)
> My mother's name -- Maria Rud (deceased)
> My mother's maiden name -- Maria Ed
> My parents had 8 children, one of which is Claus Yngve Rudd born at Louisburg, La Qua Parle County, Minnesota, U. S. A. , on June 9th, 1893. I have personal knowledge of this birth for the reason that I remember definitely that we were living at that place during the entire year in which he was born.

## Notes

- **Carl G.** on the 1941 extract is a clerk rendering of Carl J. / Carl Johan — do not invent a new given name.
- **Dora N.** ≈ Dagmar; **Alma H.** may be Hattie A. on the 1910 sheet — keep both forms; do not merge without another record.
- Albin’s **Evald** vs catalog **Edward** / Kila **Edvard** — aka, not a new person.
- Both parents **deceased** by 30 Jul 1941. Tightens Carl’s death bracket already opened by 1940 absence (**FT-0841**). Still no stone or civil death row.
- Corona address **423 East 8th Street** sits on the same 8th / Joy corner the obituary remembers.
