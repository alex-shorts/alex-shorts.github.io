## Catalog note

Newspaper death notice for Isaac Olsen (d. 18 Oct 1947). Names wife; sons Carl and William; daughters including **Mrs. C. Y. Rudd** (Corona, Calif.) = Della × Clause/Claus Rudd. Image binary: `collection/people/isaac-olsen/media/newspaper-death-notice-fag-2023.jpeg`.

## Full text

OLSEN — On Oct. 18, 1947, Isaac Olsen of 602 Garden City Rd., Lulu Is., age 89 years. Survived by his loving wife; 2 sons, Carl, Lulu Is., William, Tholclin, Alta.; 4 daughters, Mrs. D. Fitzer, Holden, Alta., Mrs. C. Y. Rudd, Corance, Calif., Mrs. L. E. Williams, Lulu Is., Mrs. J. R. McLaughlin, Vancouver. Funeral Tuesday, Oct. 21 at 1:30 p.m., from T. Edwards Memorial Chapel, Granville and 10th, Rev. J. W. Robinson officiating. Interment Mountain View Cemetery.

## Notes

Primary subject: Isaac Olsen. “Corance” = Corona (typo in print). Daughter Mrs. C. Y. Rudd corroborates Della (Olson) Rudd × Claus Y. Rudd. Daughter **Mrs. J. R. McLaughlin, Vancouver** = **Anna Augusta Olson McLachlin** (McLachlin spelling on later notices). Daughter **Mrs. L. E. Williams, Lulu Island** = **Pearl Olson Williams**. Daughter **Mrs. D. Fitzer, Holden** = OCR for **Mrs. D. Sizer** (Mabel; Jennie 1956 prints **Sizen**). Other daughters/sons soft-ingested — do not climb spouses from this object alone.
