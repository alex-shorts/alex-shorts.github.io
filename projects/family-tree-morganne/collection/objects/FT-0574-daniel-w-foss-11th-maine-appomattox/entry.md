**Military context for kids.** Proposed great-great-grandfather **Daniel W. Foss** (father of **Ambrose**, grandfather of **Harriet B. Foss Parsons**) served as a **Private in Company K, 11th Maine Infantry**, enlisting **8 December 1864** and mustering out **7 June 1865** at Norfolk, Virginia (FAG/WikiTree — not independently imaged this pass). ## Regimental late-war narrative

NPS Civil War battle unit detail (11th Maine Infantry):

> Siege operations against Petersburg and Richmond **June 16, 1864, to April 2, 1865** … Moved to Hatcher's Run **March 27–29**. **Appomattox Campaign March 28-April 9**. Assault and capture of **Forts Gregg and Baldwin** and fall of Petersburg **April 2**. Pursuit of Lee to Appomattox **April 3–9**. Rice's Station **April 6**. High Bridge **April 7**. **Appomattox Court House, April 9**. Surrender of Lee and his army.

Captain Henry C. Adams, 11th Maine — OR report **27 Mar–9 Apr 1865** (Appomattox campaign excerpt):

> … we advanced across the field to within thirty rods of the enemy's battery under a very heavy fire … As soon as we arrived there it was understood that **General Lee had surrendered**. We formed and moved by the right flank into the woods …
