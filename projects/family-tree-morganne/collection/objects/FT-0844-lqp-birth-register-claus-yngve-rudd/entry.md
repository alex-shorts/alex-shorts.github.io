## Catalog note

**Legal vital.** Certified copy of the birth register for **Claus Yngve Rudd**, born **9 June 1893**, Village of **Louisburg**, Lac qui Parle County, Minnesota. Names father **Carl Johan Rudd** (Sweden, farmer) and mother **Maria Ed** (Sweden). Certified **6 October 1941** — seventeen days after Della’s delayed filing (**FT-0843**).

Middle **Yngve** is the civil form behind WWI-draft **Yugue**, family **Young**, stone **Y.**, and ELCA **Jorgen** (**FT-0508** / **FT-0510**). Mother **Ed** matches the locked Swedish soldatnamn (**maria_edison_rudd**).
