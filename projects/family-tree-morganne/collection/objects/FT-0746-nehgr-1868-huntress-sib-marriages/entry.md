## Catalog note

Tuttle’s Newington **marriage** transcript (NEHGR 22) names three of Darling’s siblings. Supports **C2756**. FAG parent/self memorials for this generation **not found** (**C2758**).

## Full text

Christopher Huntris of N. and Elizabeth Pearson of Hampton.

Joseph Lord of Berwick and Martha nuntris of N.

Samuel Rawlins and Mary Huntris.

## Notes

- Dates in the 1868 run sit in the early-1750s block (Athenaeum: Christopher Jr. 22 Sep 1752; Martha 28 Sep 1750; Mary 12 Apr 1753). Exact day not always adjacent in OCR.
- Martha OCR “nuntris” = Huntris.
- **FAG negative:** no memorial located for Darling Huntress (1730–1815, South Berwick) or Lieut. Christopher Huntress (1703–1782, Newington). Cemetery “Mary Huntress Burial Site” (FAG cemetery 2615084, Avery Road, Newington) is a place name only — do not assign to Mary Chick without a named stone.
- Darling baptism itself is **FT-0742** (NHGR vol. 3), not this Tuttle marriage installment.
