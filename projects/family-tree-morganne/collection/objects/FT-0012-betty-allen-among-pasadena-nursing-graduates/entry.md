## Catalog note

Nursing graduation notice for Miss Betty Allen, daughter of Mr. and Mrs. R. P. Allen of Riverside. Poly high class of 1927; Pasadena Hospital training.

## Full text

BETTY ALLEN AMONG PASADENA GRADUATES

Miss Betty Allen, daughter of Mr. and Mrs. R. P. Allen of this city, will be among the graduates Friday afternoon at 5:30, at exercises to be held by the Pasadena Junior college and Pasadena hospital in the Rose bowl, where a colorful pageant will be presented in connection with the commencement ceremonies. Mr. and Mrs. Allen will attend the graduation.

Miss Allen, who is a graduate of Polytechnic high school with the class of 1927, served as president of her class the first year she was in training at the hospital, later being president of the student body. She plans to remain at the school until October 1 to complete a special course in surgery and administration.
