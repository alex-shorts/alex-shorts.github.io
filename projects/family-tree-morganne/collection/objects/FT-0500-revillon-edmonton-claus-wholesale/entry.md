## What the Revillon Building was (open authored narrative)

City of Edmonton Historic Resource Management, Revillon Building (10310-102 Avenue NW), designated 2023:

> The Revillon Building, completed in **1912**, is a Chicago Commercial Style structure on the eastern edge of Edmonton’s Warehouse District… The structure was built as a **department store and warehouse** for the Revillon Frères Company, one of the world’s premier fur manufacturers.

> The upper floors of the Revillon Building housed a major **processing centre** for their global fur trading operations, and afterwards, housed **wholesale activities** which established Edmonton as a trading centre of global repute. The building also accommodated the operating **railway spur** that ran to the east, allowing for direct access for deliveries and shipments from the world’s major trading centres.

Stone on the building still reads **EST. REVILLON 1723** / erected **1912**.

Source: [Edmonton historic-resource PDF](https://www.edmonton.ca/sites/default/files/public-files/historic-resource-revillon-building.pdf)

## Connection (timeline only — not a personnel file)

Open company history: Revillon opened an Edmonton wholesale warehouse in **1899**; HBC bought **54%** of the Canadian fur-trade company in **1926** and full ownership by **1936** (then Rupert’s Land Trading Company). Claus’s directory titles sit on either side of that sale (**Revillon clerk 1921** → **HBC wholesale 1931**). That is a **consistent path**, not proof he was transferred in the deal.
