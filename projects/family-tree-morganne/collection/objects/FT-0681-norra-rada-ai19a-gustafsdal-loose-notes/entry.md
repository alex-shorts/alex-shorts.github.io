## Catalog note

**C2706:** Gustafsdal is a **real place-name** in Norra Råda books even without an 1886–90 register heading. A I/19a end list notes **Gustafsdal 1878** (Per Olofsson) and **Gustafsdal 1883** (Sven Sigurd Stålhammar). **No** Karl Johan Rudström ~1869 × Britta Maria Andersson / Maria Edison + Albin on this page.

Same class: B/3 1880 utflyttning entry 42, Karl Joh. Thyberg Thorsby → **Gustafsdahl**, folio **259** (that folio is the **1880** HF book, not 20a sida 259).
