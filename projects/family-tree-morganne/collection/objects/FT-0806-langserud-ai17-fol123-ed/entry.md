## Full text

Langserud A I/17 (1856-1860), C0038179 image 149 = fol.123, farm Ed.

- Sold. Niklas Larsson Ed b. 9 Feb 1826 Svanskog.
- Hustru Anna Ersdotter b. 8 Nov 1826 Langserud.
- Son August 9 Jul 1852.
- Son Johannes 22 Jun 1854 (one pass read 22 May).
- D. Maja 5 May 1857 Langserud.
- D. Lotta 25 Nov 1860.
Note: Bor i Eldanas 1860-61.
Soldattorp is fol.123, not Ed fol.110-119.
