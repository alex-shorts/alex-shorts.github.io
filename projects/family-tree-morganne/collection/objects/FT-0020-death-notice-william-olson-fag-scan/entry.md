## Full text

OLSON — On August 21, 1963, William Olson of Holden, Alberta, age 78 years. Survived by his wife Carrie Olson; three sons, Arthur, Herbert and Harry, all of Holden; one daughter, Mrs. Alice Jones of Edmonton; four sisters, Mrs. Anna McLachlin of Vancouver, Mrs. Mabel Sizer of Holden, Mrs. Pearl Williams of India, and Mrs. C. (Della) Rudd of Riverside, California; one brother, Carl Olson of Vancouver; and two grandsons. Funeral Saturday, August 24 at 1:00 p.m. from Holden United Church, Rev. Ruxton officiating. Interment Holden Cemetery. Hoveland Funeral directors of Camrose.
