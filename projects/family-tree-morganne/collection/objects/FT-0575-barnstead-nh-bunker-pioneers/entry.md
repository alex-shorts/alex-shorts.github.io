## Catalog note

**Place color for kids.** **Harriet Newell Bunker** — proposed mother of **Ambrose Foss** and grandmother of **Harriet B. Foss Parsons** — was born in **Barnstead, New Hampshire** (FAG). Her parents soft-climb as **Joseph S. Bunker** × **Olive Otis Bunker** (**C565**). This Accession packages the town’s early **Bunker** settlers and mill history; it does **not** prove Harriet’s line to every named Bunker in the chapter or tie **Olive Otis** to Otis, Maine.

## Barnstead Bunker narrative (open authored text)

*History of Merrimack and Belknap Counties, NH* (1885), Barnstead abstracts:

> Among the early settlers were the following: … **Joseph Bunker** … **John Bunker**, **Eli Bunker** …
>
> The **first grist-mill was erected by John Bunker, about 1769**. He also built the **first saw-mill** in town.
>
> The following citizens of this town were in the old French War: John Aiken, Colonel Joseph Sinclair and **Joseph Bunker**.

War of 1812 roll includes **D. Bunker**, **Silas Bunker**, **T. Bunker**. Civil War roll lists **J.E. Bunker**, **J.N. Bunker (killed)**, and **Eli H. Foss** among Barnstead soldiers.

First meeting-house ~1760; **Parade Meeting-House** built ~1788 — the **Parade** neighborhood where later families including Joseph Bunker (1783–1830) lived and are buried.

## Supports

- Supports: **Bunker** as an early Barnstead founding surname with **mills and French War service**; plausible backdrop for Harriet’s NH birth
- Does not support: Harriet’s descent from John Bunker the miller; Olive Otis’s Otis ME connection; Daniel Foss’s NH birthplace details
