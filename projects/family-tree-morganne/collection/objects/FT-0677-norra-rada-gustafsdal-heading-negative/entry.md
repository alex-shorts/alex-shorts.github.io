## Catalog note

**C2700 / C2412:** **Gustafsdal** / Gustafsdahl is **not** a hemman/torp heading in A I/20a or 20b registers. C/7 entry 128 still names mother residence Gustafsdal (FT-0512) with HF p.47 — that page is Hafversbol in 20a (FT-0651) and **By** in 20b (FT-0680). Place-name survives in flytt/notes (FT-0681), not as a farm index row.
