## Corona lemon economy (open authored narrative)

Nita Hiltner, *Press-Enterprise* ("A Look Back: Corona once was big on lemons"), 31 Jul 2010:

> **Corona Foothill Lemon Company** was created in **1911** … largest citrus ranch in Corona at **900 acres** … grew to more than **2,300 acres** … **150 full-time employees** and shipped **750,000 boxes of fruit a year**, making Corona the **Lemon Capital of the World**.

> The ranch had **housing for its workers and a company store**. … Today, the Corona Heritage Foundation at the Corona Heritage Park and Museum operates out of the **1937 company store**.

> Local historian **Ted Taylor** said that by the early 1900s, Corona had become the lemon capital and that the **1930s saw the biggest growth in the industry**.

Source: [Press-Enterprise](https://www.pressenterprise.com/2010/07/31/a-look-back-corona-once-was-big-on-lemons/)
