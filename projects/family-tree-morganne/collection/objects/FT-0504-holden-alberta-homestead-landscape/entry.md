## Holden homestead village (open authored narrative)

Village of Holden, Heritage Management Plan, Appendix A (Context Paper):

> Homesteaders began arriving in significant numbers to claim the land in the Holden area around **1902**.

> The first train on the Grand Trunk Pacific Railway came through Holden in **1909**. The arrival of the railway allowed local farmers to ship surplus produce and livestock to outside markets… On **April 14th** of that year, the community of Holden was incorporated as a Village.

> Many early settlers in the Holden area were **Scandinavians of the Lutheran faith**… In 1910 the two congregations met and decided to form one church: the **Holden Scandinavian Lutheran Church**.

Source: [holden.ca HMP Appendix A](https://holden.ca/wp-content/uploads/2019/07/HMP-FINAL-All-Appendices.pdf)
