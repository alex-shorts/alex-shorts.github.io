## Notes

Rote 94 Södra Rud / soldatnamn Rud matches HF Soldattorpet N:o 94 (FT-0802). Carl Johan was soldier from 30 Apr 1880 per Kilabygden (FT-0812), so he is the expected 1881 occupant; the table does not name him. Långserud rote 67 Ed/Ed is the farm-name pattern behind Maria's soldatnamn — do not mint Niklas from that row. Näs kompani 1881 has other Rud rotar in other socknar (do not merge).
