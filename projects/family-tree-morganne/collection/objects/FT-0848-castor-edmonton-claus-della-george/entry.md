## Catalog note

Sepia studio portrait of three people on a dark brown textured mount. Embossed **Castor Ltd. EDMONTON**. The covering subject identifies the sitters as Claus, Della, and George (great-grandparents and grandfather on Morganne’s Rudd side). George looks school-age, consistent with the Edmonton years before the 1931 Corona move.

## Visual description

Woman seated left, dark wavy bob, dark jacket over a light lace neckline, beaded necklace, hands in lap. Man standing center-back, bald, dark three-piece suit, patterned tie, pocket square, left hand on the boy’s shoulder. Boy seated on a low bench, dark suit, bow tie, pocket square. Painted studio window backdrop and drape at left. Mount torn along the right edge.
