## Catalog note

**C2793 / C2794 / C2795:** The 1880 B/3 dest. **Gustafsdahl** cited folio **259** as the **from** page (Thyberg, Thorsby → Gustafsdahl). Opening that folio in A I/18a shows heading **Thorsby**, not Gustafsdahl. **No** Britta Maria Andersson piga + Karl Johan Rudström ~1869 household.

## Supports

- 1880 B/3 folio 259 = **Thorsby from-page**, not a Gustafsdahl farm page.
- Does **not** support Carl J. parents or Albin birth household.
