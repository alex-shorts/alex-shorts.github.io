Province of British Columbia — Provincial Board of Health — Division of Vital Statistics — Registration of Death (Form 6). Registration No. 008902 (1947-09-008902).

- Deceased: Isaac Olsen; male; married; b. 15 Dec 1857 Sweden; age 89y 10m 3d; Canadian citizen; Swedish origin; farmer (25 years; last worked 1947).
- Spouse: Jennie Anderson.
- Father: Olsen — given name not known; b. Sweden. Mother: maiden name not known; b. Sweden.
- Death: 18 Oct 1947, House No. 602 Garden City Rd., Richmond, B.C.; 22 years in municipality; 42 years in Canada.
- Cause: acute cardiac dilatation due to myocardial degeneration due to hypertension & coronary sclerosis; contributing old cerebral infarction / paralysis; natural causes.
- Burial: 22 Oct 1947, Mt. View Cemetery, Vancouver; undertaker The T. Edwards Co.
- Informant: Carl Olsen, son, 1117 Cambie Rd., Richmond; registered 21 Oct 1947.
