## Lyman County homestead wave (open authored narrative)

SDGenWeb Lyman County *About* (Barbara Speck):

> The Lyman County area was created in **1873**, and **opened up for homesteading in 1890**. It surveyed during **1891–1892**, then **organized in 1893**.

> After the railroad crossed the river Lyman County was opened up to a **wave of homesteaders from the east**, seeking free land.

U.S. census counts for the county: **2,632** in 1900 → **10,848** in 1910 (the rush Della was born into). Kennebec’s railroad town boom is usually dated **1905** — after her birth, about when the family appears in Canada.
