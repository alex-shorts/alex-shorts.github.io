## Catalog note

**Place narrative.** Marion Orciere (**Marcy’s mother**, Morganne’s grandmother) rests at **Crestlawn Memorial Park** in Riverside as **Marian E. Crabtree** (FAG 203881060). Her parents **Theodore M.** and **Rachel G. Orciere** share a dual stone at the same cemetery (FAG 203880948/996). This Accession packages the cemetery’s own founding story — a mid-century Inland Empire memorial landscape — not Marion’s remarriage trail (Crabtree/Harris).
