## Boardwalk / Ross Brothers (open authored narrative)

City of Edmonton Historic Resource Management, The Boardwalk (10310–102 Avenue NW):

> The Ross Brothers Limited Warehouse, also known as the **Boardwalk Building**, was built in three stages in **1910, 1928 and 1940**. … located in Edmonton’s **Warehouse District**, on the western edge of Downtown.

> … built as a warehouse backing onto a **railway spur line** … Earlier in its life … a **centre of commerce and trade** that established Edmonton as the major wholesale trading centre to an entire region. It was an **anchor** to what was Edmonton’s Warehouse District.

> … goods unloaded directly from **railroad cars** onto chutes and large electric freight elevators, to be weighed on industrial **Fairbanks Scales**, then stored on upper floors constructed of solid wood.

> … operating **railway spur** that ran to the immediate west, allowing railway cars direct access to the Boardwalk Building for deliveries and shipments.

Source: [Edmonton Boardwalk SoS PDF](https://www.edmonton.ca/sites/default/files/public-files/historic-resource-the-boardwalk.pdf)
