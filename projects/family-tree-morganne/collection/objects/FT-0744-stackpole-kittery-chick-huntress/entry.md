## Catalog note

Compiled Chick family block: **Mary** m. **Christopher Huntress of Newington**, intentions recorded **26 Oct 1724**. Names Mary as daughter of **Richard Chick** × **Martha Lord**. Supports **C2754**. Gen-11 parked.

## Full text

CHICK.

Richard, son of Thomas and Elizabeth (Spencer) Chick, married, 11 July 1702, Martha, dau. of Nathan and Martha (Tozier) Lord. … Children were:

Mary m. Christopher Huntress of Newington. Int Rec. 26 Oct 1724.

Richard m. Bethiah Gould 19 Jan. 1728. …
