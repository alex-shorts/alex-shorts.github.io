## Catalog note

Newington church baptisms naming **Christopher Huntris** × **Mary** as parents of **Darling** (19 Apr 1730) plus named siblings Christopher, Martha, Mary, George. Supports **C2751–C2756**. Ceiling **Probable** — published transcript, no register image.

## Full text

**1727 June** — Item Christopher Huntris & Mary his wife own'd ye Covenant & she was baptized.

**1728 Oct 13** — Item Christopher son to Christopher Huntris & Mary Huntris was baptized. Item Martha Daughter to Christopher & Mary Huntris was baptized.

**1730 April 19** — Darling son to Christopher Huntris & Mary his wife was bapt d.

**1732 May 7** — Mary Daughter to Christopher & Mary Huntris was baptized.

**1734 Feb 3** — Christopher Huntis and wife had a son baptized George.
