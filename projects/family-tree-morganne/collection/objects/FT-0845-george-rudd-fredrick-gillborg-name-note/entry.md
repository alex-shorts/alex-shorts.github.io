## Catalog note

Scrap note in block capitals on **California Land Title Company** (CLT) stationery. Dan identifies the hand as **George E. Rudd** (his father) and reads the pre-Rudd name as **Frederick Gilbert**. The letters on the paper are **FREDRICK GILLBORG**.

This is **family lore about surnames**, not a civil name-change decree. Do **not** mint a person `frederick_gillborg`. Do **not** treat the scrap as verification of the locked Swedish identity (**Karl Johan Fredriksson Rud**, born **Gillberga**, son of **Fredrik Svensson**). Those names come from parish books (**FT-0802** / **FT-0803**), not from this note. A rhyme (Fredrick / Fredriksson, Gillborg / Gillberga) is a hypothesis only — **unverified**.

## Visual description

Small vertical scrap, off-white. All-caps black ink, a few red marks under letters. Horizontal flourish between the two blocks. Printed blue/orange **CALIFORNIA LAND TITLE COMPANY** / CLT oval logo at the foot.

## Handwritten notes

Diplomatic (uncertain marks `[?]`):

```
NAME BEFORE
"RUD"
[two arrows pointing down]
"FREDRICK GILLBORG"

[flourish]

MY DAD; CLAUS V. RUDD
LEGALLY ADDED ANOTHER
"R" TO "RUD".
```

Red marks sit on **D** letters in RUD / RUDD (the extra-letter story). Dan’s covering email also says Claus “added the other **R**.” American records show **Rud → Rudd** (extra **D**). Keep both readings; do not pick a court spelling from this scrap.

**CLAUS V.** is George’s form; the 1941 birth register (**FT-0844**) is **Claus Yngve** — V is likely a Y/Yngve misread, not a new middle name.
