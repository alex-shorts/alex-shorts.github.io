## Full text

PARSONS, ELIZABETH “BETTY” A. 95, Passed away September 15, 2005 at The Seasons at Riverside. She was predeceased by her beloved husband, Jerry, and is survived by their two sons, Jack and Bill Parsons; seven grandchildren and thirteen great-grandchildren. In addition to being a devoted wife and mother, Betty was a registered nurse whose love and compassion made an enormous difference in the lives she touched for over 50 years. Private graveside services will be held at Olivewood Cemetery. In lieu of flowers, donations may be made to Inland Valley Hospice, 3770 Myers St., Riverside, CA., 92503. ACHESON & GRAHAM MORTUARY (951) 688-1221
