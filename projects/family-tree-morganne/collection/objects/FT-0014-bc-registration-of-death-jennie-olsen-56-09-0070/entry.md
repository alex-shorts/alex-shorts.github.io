## Visible text

Province of British Columbia — Registration of Death (Form 6). Reg. 56-09-007015.

- Deceased: Jennie Olsen; female; widowed; Canadian; Swedish origin; b. 11 Jul 1863 Sweden; age 92; at home.
- Husband: Isaac Olsen.
- Father / mother: not known; both born Sweden.
- Residence: 4081 Pandora St., Burnaby, B.C.
- Death: 23 Jun 1956, Vancouver General Hospital; 2 years in municipality of death, 31 years in province, 50 years in Canada.
- Cause: hypostatic pneumonia (4 days) due to cachexia & senility; other: carcinoma of colon.
- Burial: 26 Jun 1956, Mt. View Cemetery, Vancouver; undertaker The T. Edwards Co. Ltd.
- Informant: Carl Olsen, son, 1117 Cambie Rd., Lulu Island; signed 25 Jun 1956; registered Vancouver 27 Jun 1956.
