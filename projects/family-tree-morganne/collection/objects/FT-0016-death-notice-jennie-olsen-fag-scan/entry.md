## Catalog note

Newspaper death notice for Jennie Olsen (d. 23 Jun 1956). Names daughters including **Mrs. C. Y. Rudd**; sons Carl and William. Image binary: `collection/people/jennie-olsen/media/newspaper-death-notice-fag-2023.jpeg`.

## Full text

OLSEN—On June 23, 1956, Jennie Olsen, formerly of 4081 Pandora St., in her 93rd year. Survived by 4 daughters, Mrs. C. Y. Rudd, Mrs. D. Sizen, Mrs. L. E. Williams, Mrs. J. R. McLachlin; 2 sons, Carl and William. Private funeral service was held on Tuesday, June 26, at 1:30 p.m. in the T. Edward Memorial Chapel, Granville and 10th Ave. Rev. J. E. Zackareas officiated. Interment, Mountain View Cemetery.
