## What HBC Wholesale was (open authored narrative)

### Marcia Stentz, *Manitoba History* 63 (Spring 2010)

> A logical extension of HBC’s historic role as merchanttrader, **Hudson’s Bay Wholesale** broadened HBC’s role from supplier of its own business (fur posts and saleshops) to supplier of goods to **non-HBC retailers including hotels, restaurants, gas stations and grocers**. Merchandise carried by the department included goods that had long been important to the fur trade: dry goods, hardware, liquor, tea and coffee, tobacco and point blankets.

> Initially an outgrowth of the saleshop business, Wholesale was established as a department by **1928** and it went through periods of growth and decline in the context of immigration, crop yields, liquor laws and wars.

Source: [Manitoba Historical Society](https://www.mhs.mb.ca/docs/mb_history/63/hudsonsbay.shtml)

### WPGxHBC caption (HBCA photo series)

> This 1921 photograph shows the sales office for Hudson’s Bay Company’s Wholesale Department. … starting in **1907**, the HBC operated a profitable wholesale business that supplied goods to **local town shops across the West**. Main operations were based in Winnipeg and goods carried included groceries, hardware, footwear, stationary, liquor, tea packing and coffee roasting.

Source: [cargocollective.com/wpghbc/C-5-1](https://cargocollective.com/wpghbc/C-5-1)
