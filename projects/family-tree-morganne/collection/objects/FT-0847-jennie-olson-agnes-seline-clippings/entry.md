## Catalog note

Three yellowed clippings on one page. They are **two different women**. The covering subject line treated Jeannie / Jennie as also known as Agnes Seline Olson. The paper does not: **Jennie Olson** is Della’s mother (nearly 91; funeral names **Mrs. Della Rudd of California**). **Agnes Seline Olson**, 54, d. 17 Jan 1962, is wife of **Carl** — Della’s sister-in-law (`carl_leonard_olson` / Agnes Bicknell on FT-0487), not Della’s mother.

Related short death notice already on file: **FT-0016** (FAG scan, Olsen spelling). This Accession is a longer Holden-area funeral write-up plus the Agnes pair.
