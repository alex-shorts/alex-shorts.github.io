## Olivewood founding & character (open authored narrative)

### Olivewood Memorial Park (home)

> In the mid 1880’s, local businessman **Charles O. Perrine** galvanized efforts at establishing a large, better-maintained cemetery in Riverside. After a slow start, Perrine’s plan to use **75 acres** he owned near **Pachappa Hill** was given a significant boost following a **June 1887** editorial in the *Riverside Daily Press*.

Source: [olivewoodcem.com](https://olivewoodcem.com/)

### Ken Crawford, Raincross Gazette (2025)

> This large memorial park, established in **1888**, offers a unique, tangible connection to Riverside’s past. … Unlike Evergreen Cemetery, which sits Downtown … Olivewood often goes unnoticed. Though much larger, it's a place many pass quickly on **Central Avenue**.

> While not as famous as its Downtown counterpart, Olivewood was founded with an **endowment for grave maintenance**.

Source: [Raincross Gazette](https://www.raincrossgazette.com/olivewood-cemetery-tour-a-unique-way-to-hear-riversides-story/)

### Asian American Riverside (UCR)

> Olivewood Memorial Park is one of the two oldest cemeteries in Riverside … It was founded in **1888** by local pioneer families and was originally on the outskirts of Riverside, surrounded by agricultural land. It is now on either side of Central Avenue, just east of the I-91 Freeway, and is operated by a non-profit foundation.

Source: [asianamericanriverside.ucr.edu](https://asianamericanriverside.ucr.edu/sites/OlivewoodMemorialPark/index.html)
