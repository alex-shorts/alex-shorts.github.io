## Catalog note

Later Newington baptisms of **Noah** and **Elizabeth**, children of **Christopher & Mary Huntris**, plus Darling × Love owning the covenant (identity continuity after the 1730 baptism). Supports **C2756**.

## Full text

**1742/3 Feb. 20** — Noah Son to Christopher & Mary Huntris was baptized. (NHGR 4:16)

**1743/4 March 18** — Elizabeth Daughter to Christopher Huntris & Mary his wife was Baptized. (NHGR 4:17)

**Also 1744** — Christopher Huntris Junr was admited a member in full Communion in this Church.

**Octr 29** (New Style, later run) — Darling Huntris & Love his wife own'd the Covenant.

**Nov 12th** — Mary Daughter to Darling & Love Huntris was baptized.

**Later** — Darling & Love Huntris had a Daughter baptized Hannah.

## Notes

- Darling/Love covenant lines corroborate the 1730 Newington Darling with the Berwick 1751 marriage (Love Hearl).
- Mark Huntress (Athenaeum-only, same winter as Elizabeth) **not** in this transcript — not minted.
