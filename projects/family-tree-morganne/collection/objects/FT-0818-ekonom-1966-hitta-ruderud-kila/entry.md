## Story-usable (no invented color)

The American surname Rudd begins with Carl Johan Fredriksson. He enlisted 30 April 1880 as soldat on Kila socken soldattorp 94 (Södra Rud / Ruderud, Värmland) after Anders Andersson Rud died 17 January 1880, and took the soldatnamn Rud. Earlier generations on this line used patronymics only.

The croft is the place called Ruderud under hemman Rud in Kila socken, Näs härad. In 2018 the Kila hembygdsförening still knew it: after coffee, walking the big road toward the start, one passed Pellerud on a height, then Edmanskerud on the right a few tens of meters in the woods, then “soldattorpet i Rud, till vänster invid vägen. Soldattorpet Ruderud är liksom Pellerud välskött.” Tallmorönningen — Ingersbyn “20 öre” — lay on the other side of the road at Ruderud.

That walk is westbound on the forest road. Edmanskerud (about 59.19707, 12.71849) is about 30 meters north of the road — the right side when walking west. Ruderud is therefore on the left: south of the road, immediately beside it, after Edmanskerud. On the 1966 Ekonomiska kartan sheet 10 C 3 e Ulvsbol the name Ruderud is printed next to a building just south of INGERSBYN / Tallmon and west of Pellerud. The 2026 Hitta visiting address “Rud Ruderud,” 661 94 Säffle, district Kila, is at 59.196102, 12.717034 — about 10 meters left of the road and about 136 meters southwest of Edmanskerud. That is a labeled address and a printed map name. It is not a surveyed pin on a house corner.
