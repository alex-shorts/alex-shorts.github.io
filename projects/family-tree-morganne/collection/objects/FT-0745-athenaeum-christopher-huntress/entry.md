## Catalog note

Newington compiled catalog: Christopher Huntress mason, b. **19 Apr 1703** Portsmouth, d. **6 Aug 1782** probably Newington; spouse **Mary Chick** (dau. Richard Chick × Martha Lord, b. c1703 Kittery, m. 1724); child **3. Darling Huntress of Berwick, bp. 19 Apr 1730**, m. 6 Dec 1751 Love Earle. Child list used for named-sibling soft-ingest. Supports **C2751–C2757**. **Mark** (item 10) not minted — absent from NHGR extracts and from WT will list.

## Full text

Name: Huntress, Christopher, 1703-1782
Born: Apr. 19, 1703 — Portsmouth, N.H.
Deceased: Aug. 6, 1782 — prob. Newington, N.H.
Places of residence: Newington, N.H.
Occupation: Mason.
Father: George? Huntress.
Spouse: Mary Chick, daughter of Richard Chick and Martha Lord, b. c1703 in Kittery, Me., m. 1724.

Children — All bp. in Newington, N.H.

1. Christopher Huntress, bp. Oct. 13, 1728, m. Sept. 22, 1752 to Elizabeth Pearson.
2. Martha Huntress, bp. Oct. 13, 1728, m. Sept. 28, 1750 to Joseph Lord of Berwick, Me.
3. Darling Huntress, of Berwick, Me., bp. Apr. 19, 1730, m. Dec. 6, 1751 to Love Earle, daughter of Etherington Earle and Hannah Goodwin.
4. Mary Huntress, bp. May 7, 1732, m. Apr. 12, 1753 to Lawrence Rollins, son of Samuel Rollins and Alice Dame.
5. George Huntress, bp. Feb. 3, 1734.
6. David Huntress, bp. Mar. 13, 1737.
7. Nathan Huntress, b. c1739.
8. Noah Huntress, bp. Feb. 20, 1742, m. Abigail Pickering, daughter of John Pickering and Mary Nutter.
9. Elizabeth Huntress, bp. Mar. 18, 1743/44, m. ____ Stevens.
10. Mark Huntress, bp. Feb. 20, 1743/44.
