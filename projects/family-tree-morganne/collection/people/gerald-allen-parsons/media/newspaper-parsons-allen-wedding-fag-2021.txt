PARSONS-ALLEN VOWS EXCHANGED
Riverside Couple Plight Troth at Wee Kirk O' The Heather

Wee Kirk o' the Heather in Glendale was the setting for the marriage yesterday afternoon at 5 o'clock of Miss Elizabeth Leonard Allen, daughter of Mr. and Mrs. Raymond P. Allen of 5222 Magnolia avenue, and Gerald Austin Parsons, son of Mrs. N. B. Parsons of 5110 Magnolia avenue. Some 60 friends were present to witness the exchange of vows, for which Rev. Ezra James Egly, pastor of Calvary Presbyterian church, officiated.

The bride was attractive in a gown of white chiffon velvet, her long tulle veil being caught to her head in a cap of Chantilly lace. She carried a shower bouquet of gardenias, lilies of the valley and orchids. She was given in marriage by her father.

Miss Carolyn Barrows of Pasadena was maid of honor, wearing a yellow gown, with which she carried a sheaf of Talisman roses. Harold A. Parsons of Riverside served his brother as best man. Harry Hicks and Tracy Prichard of Riverside, brothers-in-law of the bridegroom, were ushers.

Preceding the entrance of the bridal party Mrs. Harold Parsons sang "I Love You Truly" and "At Dawning."

Mothers of both young people wore navy blue, Mrs. Allen's gown being set off with a corsage of yellow roses, while Mrs. Parsons wore pink roses. Miss Edna Bailey of Riverside and Miss Mary McKinstry of Hollywood, who gave out boxes of wedding cake after the ceremony, had corsages of gardenias, as did Mrs. Harold Parsons.

After the ceremony Mr. and Mrs. Parsons left on a motor trip, planning to return to Riverside to make their home.

Both young people are graduates of Polytechnic high school. Later the bride was graduated from nurses' training school in Pasadena. At the time of her marriage she was surgical nurse at the children's hospital in Hollywood. Mr. Parsons, who is associated with his brother, Harold A. Parsons, in business, is well-known as star pitcher for Riverside's nightball team.
