## Summary

**Confirmed** as mother of oäkta **Maja/Maria** 23 Apr 1827 (C-book names her). Own birth not locked. Tollersrud HF p.199 has a staying piga **Cajsa Nilsdotter b. 7 Sep 1794 Värmskog** — best candidate, not locked. **FT-0850:** Värmskog C/6 Födde **1794** has **no** Cajsa Nilsdotter at all (7 Sep = twin Eriksson boys; Aug 30 twin Caisa died 1795; Nov Kajsa is Larsdotter). A 1807 Cajsa left for Borgvik in 1826.
