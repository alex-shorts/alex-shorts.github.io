## Summary

Proposed mother of **Darling Huntress**. Soft-ingest **Probable** / `verified: false` — pack **C2752**. Maiden **Chick**. Stackpole: daughter of **Richard Chick** × **Martha Lord**; intentions **26 Oct 1724** to Christopher Huntress of Newington. Athenaeum: b. **c1703** Kittery (WT 1705). Newington church names her with Christopher as parents of Darling. Death date unknown. Gen-11 Richard × Martha — **parked C2759**. **No FAG / no open obituary**. Distinct from daughter-in-law Love Hearl and from later Mary Huntress burial-site cemetery.
