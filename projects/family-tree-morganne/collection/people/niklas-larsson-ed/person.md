## Summary

**Confirmed** father of **Maria Nikolausdotter Ed** (`maria_edison_rudd`) from the Långserud 1857 C-book. Soldatnamn **Ed** — that is the Edison/Edeson in US indexes. Birth **9 Feb 1826 Svanskog** on both C-book and later HF.

**OPEN conflict:** C-book father **Lars Hansson** × **Maria Andersdotter age 45** (~1781). Gaterud HF p.272 lists a Niclas 9 Feb 1826 under **Nils Persson** (1793) × **Cajsa Nilsdotter** (1810), with enk. **Maria Andersdotter b. 1766**. Same date and farm; different father and mother age. Parent links stay **Probable / not accepted**. Do not merge.
