## Summary

Probable mother of Samuel D. Shorts (**C71**, Wave 2). Hannah Ellenor (Pringle / Prindle) Shorts, 1776 New York -- 1862 Fredericksburgh. Wife of Rev Augustus Shorts. Buried Sexsmith Cemetery, Napanee ON -- same cemetery as Samuel. **C210** soft-ingest: parents **William Pringle** × **Eunice Benedict** (Probable / `verified: false`) — hardens Ontario cluster **C132**. Sister of Rev Andrew Prindle (FAN).
