## Summary

Probable father of Mary Ann Denison Shorts (**C65**, Wave 2). Nathan (Nathaniel) Denison, b. **2 Mar 1781** Stonington CT; m. Elizabeth Thompson **15 Dec 1803** Berne, Albany Co. NY (**C133**); settled Napanee / Richmond Twp; d. **16 Nov 1849**. Buried Riverview Cemetery, Napanee. Baldwin 1881 lists daughter Mary Ann m. Samuel D. Shorts; nine-child FAN includes Daniel × Hannah Shorts Hughes (**C136**).

**Ascent refill:** parents soft-ingested **Robert Denison** × **Anna (Chesebrough) Denison** (**C220**, `verified: false`) from Baldwin **p. 147** + Wheeler **1900**.
