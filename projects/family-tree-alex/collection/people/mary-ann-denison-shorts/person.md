## Summary

Mother of Rev Alexander Shorts (**C34 Confirmed**). Mary Ann Denison Shorts 1809–1872; wife of Samuel Shorts. FAG-linked children Alexander + Robert (**C52**); Baldwin/FAG also list Elizabeth / Jane / Andrew / Hannah (**C53 Possible**). Probable parents Nathan Denison × Elizabeth Thompson of Napanee (**C65–C66**, Wave 2 — pending Alex Y/N). Buried Sexsmith Cemetery, Napanee (FAG; no stone photo). Museum quilt 2014.36.01 is Denison FAN lore (FT-0055) — 1890 “Mrs Mary Denison Aged 50” inscription is unlikely to be this Mary Ann if 1872/1874 death holds.
