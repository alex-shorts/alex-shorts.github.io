## Summary

Father of Rev Dorland Shorts (**C33 Confirmed**). Rev Alexander Shorts, 1839 – 17 May 1896. Methodist Episcopal minister; founder of the Grindstone Island church; buried Thurso Cemetery on Grindstone Island. Spouse Eva Eliza Aylesworth; parents Samuel Shorts × Mary Ann Denison (**C34 Confirmed**). Brother **Robert Shorts** (**C52 Probable**).
