## Summary

Paternal grandfather of Alexander Martin Shorts. Born Rochester, NY; family to Southern California 1929; South Gate High School 1941; WWII U.S. Army Air Corps (Operations Specialist; Panama); later fireman (Arcadia → Huntington Park → Burbank Captain). Married Gloria Gatz 22 Oct 1950 (59 years).

### Military records to pull

**Check first (ranked):**

1. **Archival OMPF (NPRC / NARA)** — discharged 1945 → public archival (62+ years). Order via [eVetRecs](https://www.archives.gov/veterans/military-service-records) or [SF-180](https://www.archives.gov/veterans/military-service-records/standard-form-180); fees typically $25 (≤5 pp) / $70 (6+ pp). Hope for: serial number, enlistment/separation dates, character of discharge, units, MOS, training (Victorville), Panama assignment. **1973 fire risk** for Army/AAF 1912–1964 — may get reconstruction only.
2. **WWII draft card (young men’s, not Old Man’s)** — b. 1922 → Ancestry/Fold3/FamilySearch *U.S. WWII Draft Cards Young Men*; or [NARA St. Louis Selective Service](https://www.archives.gov/st-louis/selective-service) ($7 card). Hope for: 1940–42 address (South Gate / LA), employer, physical desc., contact person.
3. **NARA AAD enlistment** — [fielded search](https://aad.archives.gov/aad/fielded-search.jsp?dt=893&cat=WR26&tf=F) (Enlistment) + [Reserve Corps](https://aad.archives.gov/aad/fielded-search.jsp?dt=929&cat=WR26&tf=F). **Tried 2026-08-06:** `SHORTS` + year of birth `22` → 3 hits (Harry F, Melvin R, Ernest G) — **no Richard**. File is incomplete; absence ≠ no service.
4. **Fold3 / Ancestry** — same enlistment + draft images (paywall; free at many libraries / NARA). Search after OMPF gives serial # / unit.
5. **Morning reports (AAF / Panama)** — archival; [in-person St. Louis or independent researcher only](https://www.archives.gov/personnel-records-center/military-personnel/morning-reports-and-unit-rosters-access) (no mail). Needs unit designation from OMPF first.
6. **County-filed discharge (CA)** — some recorders hold DD-214 copies; access limited ([Gov. Code 6107](https://leginfo.legislature.ca.gov/faces/codes_displaySection.xhtml?lawCode=GOV&sectionNum=6107.)). Optional LA / Ventura check; no useful statewide WWII bonus index found.
