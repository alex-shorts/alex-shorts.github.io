## Summary

Father of Rev Alexander Shorts (**C34 Confirmed**). Samuel D. Shorts 1809-1869; married Mary Ann Denison 10 Apr 1832 (per FAG bio + Baldwin 1881). FAG-linked children: Alexander (1839-1896) and Robert (1849-1929, **C52**). Bio/Baldwin additional children Elizabeth / Andrew / Hannah / Jane (**C53 Possible** — not promoted). **Wave 2:** probable parents Rev Augustus Shorts x Hannah Ellenor Pringle (**C70-C71**); 1851 Richmond census shows Agustis+Ellenor empty nest (Samuel absent from township index). Birth Richmond Twp; burial Sexsmith Cemetery Napanee with parents' cluster.
