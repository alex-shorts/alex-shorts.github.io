## Catalog note

**Secondary / local-historian.** Compiled Richardson County narrative: (1) Falls City’s **Die Deutsche Gesellschaft** (organized 17 Dec 1877) — German-language civic association active while Johann Ludwig lived with his son in town; (2) **Arago** as a German-laid town that by the 1880s was “the center of a thickly settled farming community.” Ambient only — no Hilgenfeld surname in these Andreas passages.

## Full text

**Die Deutsche Gesellschaft** (*Andreas*, Richardson Co. Part 3 — Falls City Societies):

> *Die Deutsche Gesellschaft*.—The Germans of Falls City, Neb., have organized an association under the title or name as “Die Deutsche Gesellschaft of Falls City, Neb.” It began on the 17th day of December, 1877, with a membership of forty persons—all Germans. The association now numbers about fifty members and owns real estate in the city 100 x 120 feet, with a commodious brick building thereon for the use of the association. The main object of the association is to cultivate, develope and propagate the pure German language, philosophy and character; conducive to make themselves and their countrymen fit patriots of their adopted country, and to know and uphold justice and liberty.

**Arago** (*Andreas*, Richardson Co. Part 15):

> Arago, located on the Missouri, a short distance above St. Stephens, was formerly the property of Stephen Story, and was sold by him to a party of Germans who laid it out as a town. … At the present time it is a post office, the center of a thickly settled farming community.
