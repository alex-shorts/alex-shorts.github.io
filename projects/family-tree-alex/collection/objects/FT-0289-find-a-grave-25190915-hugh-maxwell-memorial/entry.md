## Full text

**Memorial header / vitals**

- Name: Hugh Maxwell (Veteran)
- Birth: **8 Apr 1701** — County Tyrone, Northern Ireland
- Death: **19 Mar 1759** (aged 57) — Bedford, Middlesex County, Massachusetts, USA
- Burial: Old Burying Ground, Bedford, Middlesex County, Massachusetts, USA — Plot Sect. H2, Stone 327
- Memorial ID: **25190915**

**Inscription (headstone)**

> HERE LYES THE BODY OF MR HUGH MAXWEL HE DE-PARTED THIS LIFE MARCH 19 1759 IN THE 59 YEAR OF HIS AGe MY BODY TURND INTO DUST MY DUST IT SHALL ARISE IN RESURECTION OF THE JUST TO SOUND JEHOVAH'S PRAISE.

**Biography on memorial (excerpt)**

> Bedford deaths: MAXWELL, Hugh, Mar. 19, 1759, a. 59. G.R. "He came from Belfast, Ire.; was killed by a fall from his horse." (Brown's History of Bedford)

> Hugh, b. near Belfast, Ire., left his country because of religious persecution, and settled in Bedford in 1732. He d. 1759. His wife d. 1769.

> From Barbara Krall: Hugh Maxwell b. April 8, 1701 in Minterburn, County Tyrone, Ireland … married Sarah Corbett, June 3, 1728 … Benjamin Butler Maxwell, Lieu. 3-17-1737 Bedford MA d. 2-2-1829 Heath, MA …

**Family links shown on memorial**

- Spouse: **Sarah Corbett Maxwell**
- Child: **LTC Benjamin Butler Maxwell Sr** (among others)
