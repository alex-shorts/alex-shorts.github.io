## Catalog note

Two **delayed birth certificate** index entries from Mills Co. Book 1 name **Hugh D. Anderson** and **Urtha** Stonebraker as parents of **Azalia Gaile** (1904, Henderson) and **Ursula Lucille** (1913, Emerson).

## Full text

**ANDERSON, Azalia Gaile**, F, ___, **09 Aug 1904**, Henderson — **Hugh D. ANDERSON**, 26, IA — **Urtha Almina STONEBRAKER**, 23, IA (application **11 Jan 1943**).

**ANDERSON, Ursula Lucille**, F, 3rd, **16 Jul 1913**, Emerson — **Hugh D. ANDERSON**, 35, IA — **Urtha A. STONEBRAKER**, 32, IA (application **21 Dec 1943**).

Source: Clerk's Office, Mills County Court House, Glenwood, Iowa (IAGenWeb; Beverly Boileau / Cay Merryman transcripts).

## Notes

- Confirms maiden **Stonebraker** and given-name variants **Urtha Almina** / **Urtha A.** on legal delayed filings.
- Parent ages imply Hugh b. ~1877–78 IA and Urtha b. ~1880–81 IA (soft, already on person nodes).

### Supports

- Supports: Hugh D. Anderson as father of Azalia Gaile and Ursula Lucille; Urtha Stonebraker maiden name and forename spellings on delayed births.
- Does not support: Hugh or Urtha parents; exact birth dates for Hugh/Urtha; death/burial.
