## Catalog note

Contemporary **1876** Westfield local history names **E. J. Buck, M.D.** as a **“first class physician and a gentleman,”** present from fall **1855** until army service **1862**, briefly returning after the war then choosing **Platteville** as a **“larger field.”** Reputation/escapade color not in FT-0200 or FT-0360.

## Full text

From “WESTFIELD—Early Settlement and Present Make-up in 1876,” *The Montello Express*, 24 Jun 1876 (WIGenWeb transcript):

> E. J. BUCK, M. D., came among us in the fall of 1855. He was a first class physician and a gentleman in every sense of the word. He remained with us until 1862, when he entered the U. S. Army. He lived to return to us after the war closed, but he stopped with us a few days only, preferring a larger field in which to labor. He is now, and has been for several years, at Platteville, Wis. For several years past we have had H. RUSSELL and S. H. DALY as our practicing physicians.
