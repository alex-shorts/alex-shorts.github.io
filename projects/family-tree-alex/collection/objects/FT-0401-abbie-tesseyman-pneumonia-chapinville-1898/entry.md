## Catalog note

Death notice for **Mrs. Abbie Tesseyman**, Chapinville, d. **22 Dec 1898**, pneumonia, ae **78**. Names five surviving children including **Mrs. Ann Merritt** of Canandaigua — corroborates Abigail = mother of Ann L. Merritt. **Does not** name Abbie's parents or maiden surname (**C1633**).

## Full text

From **Ontario County Journal**, **30 December 1898** (GenWeb Ta–Te obituaries):

> Chapinville, N. Y. - Mrs. **Abbie Tesseyman**, an old resident of this place, died suddenly at her home on Thursday, **Dec. 22**, of **pneumonia**, aged **78** years. She leaves five children, **Robert** and **William** of Buffalo; **Mrs. Ann Merritt** of Canandaigua; **Mrs. A. O. Squires** of Chicago; and **Ethelinda Tesseyman** of this place, all of whom were here to attend the funeral, which was held on Saturday afternoon.
