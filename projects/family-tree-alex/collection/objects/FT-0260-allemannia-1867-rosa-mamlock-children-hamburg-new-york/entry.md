## Catalog note

**Immigration story for Mamie's line.** Before Mamie married Monroe Cohn in 1893, her mother **Rosa Mamlock** brought four children from Germany on the **Allemannia** (Hamburg, 9 Sep 1867). Mamie herself was **born in New York City** on **1 May 1872** — a second-generation New Yorker in the marriage cert sense, though her parents were immigrants.
