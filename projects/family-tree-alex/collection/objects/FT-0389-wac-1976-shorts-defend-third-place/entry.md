**Defense of 1975 title.** Previews list Shorts among defending individual champs trying to repeat. Results: **“The 1975 champion in the backstroke, Bill Shorts of Utah, failed to repeat** as Arizona’s Ken DeMont … win in :53.7. **Shorts was third at :54.2**.” Companion page notes DeMont’s 200-back domination; Shorts sixth at 1:59.7 in related backstroke coverage.
