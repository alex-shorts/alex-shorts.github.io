## Full text

**Otho Nial (4)** — from Arthur Aylsworth 1887 **p. 89** (Homer E. Aylsworth):

> Married **1805**, **Charlotte Bowerman**, born **1779**, **daughter of David Bowerman**; dwelt Ernesttown, Lennox County, Ontario; he died **1821**, she **1869**.

Child list on same page includes **Jonathan Fairfield (5)**, born **1812**, married Margaret Gilchrist — restated on p. **160** as Jonathan son of Otho Nial.

**Compiled vitals cross-check** (Bowerman appendix / aylesworth.net — not in 1887 body):

- Charlotte b. **16 Oct 1779** (Hallowell); d. **27 Mar 1869** (Hillier); burial McFaul Cemetery (compiled)
