## Catalog note

**1974 WAC championships (day 1).** Utah won the **400 medley relay**. **Bill Shorts** swam the **backstroke lead-off**, handing to Brian Smith (breast), Jeff Rolan (fly), Gary Anderson (free). Tribune: team “already has qualified for the NCAA meet.” Deseret: Shorts “started off in the backstroke and handed Brian Smith a lead.”
