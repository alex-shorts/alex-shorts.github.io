## Excerpts

### Grant + KP War captaincy

> In 1654, Captain George Denison and his second wife Ann Borodell Denison, often referred to as Lady Ann, brought their family to live on the land granted to him by Pequot Plantation in 1652. From there, Captain George went forward as a farmer, surveyor, selectman, deputy to the General Court in Hartford and a captain in the Great Narragansett (King Phillips) War. His war service earned him other substantial land grants.

### Manor fire and 1717 house

> In 1663 Captain George raised his great manor house at Pequotsepos… In 1717 that manor house burned down and the current house was built by their grandson Captain George Denison and his wife Lucy Gallup Denison. Since it's construction in 1717 six continuous generations have called this stately manor house their home.

### Society / museum (evolution)

> Ann Borodell Denison Gates… incorporated the George and Ann Borodell Denison Society in January of 1930… bequeathed the 1717 house… Following the end of World War II, the Denison Society embarked upon the mission of creating the Denison Homestead Museum.
