## Full text

Mrs. Glendora Blakely, 81, former Bloomington city health supervising nurse, died at her home at La Mesa, Calif., Saturday. She had been ill since March. Her funeral will be in California.

Mrs. Blakely was city nurse from 1933 to June 1941, when she moved to California. She returned in August, 1942, and remained until she returned to California in 1944.

She is survived by a daughter, Mrs. Kathleen Anderson of La Mesa, a sister, Miss Lillian Blakely of Platteville, Wis., and a grandson.
