**Published meet report + photo caption.** Oregon won the Utah Development meet; Utah fourth. Names **Bill Shorts** placing **second in the 100 backstroke** (finals day) and **third in the 200 backstroke**. Caption: “Ute backstroker, Bill Shorts, stroking his way to third place in the 200 backstroke Saturday.” Also: “Shorts, a high school backstroker now swimming for the Utes.” Photo credit on page: Steve Schowengerdt (shared sports page).
