## Catalog note

Death notice for **Ida L. Achilles Tuxhorn** — mother of **Martha Tuxhorn Gatz** (Gloria’s mother). Names six surviving children including Martha in Bellflower, Calif. FAG bio preserves a typo (“1886” for birth year); McPherson marriage index and person vitals hold **29 Aug 1866** and marriage **8 Jan 1891**.

## Full text

Mrs. Ida L. Tuxhorn, 84, widow of Leo Tuxhorn, died in McPherson hospital at 3 a.m. Tuesday one day after her 61st wedding anniversary.

She was born in Lee county, Ia., Aug. 29, 1886, came to the Inman community with her parents when she was nine years old. She was married Jan. 8, 1890 and her husband died Jan. 21, 1931.

Survivors are a son, Henry, Thayer, Kas.; five daughters, Mrs. Carolyn Williams, Cimarron, Mrs. Florence Graves, Inman, Mrs. Martha Gatz, Bellflower, Calif., Mrs. Ruth Straka, McPherson and Mrs. William Prather, St. Louis.

The funeral will be at 2:30 p.m. Friday in the Evangelical and Reformed church of which she was a member. Burial will be in North Inman cemetery. Rev. William Jasaman will officiate.

The Hutchinson News (Hutchinson, Kansas) 10 Jan 1951, Wed Page 20.
