## Catalog note

**Benham→Prindle story hinge** for Shorts UEL ascent: Nathan's daughter **Jemima** marries Joel³ **1718** (Samuel Bishop); after Joel's early death **1726**, Jemima m. **John Morgan 1729**; Nathan's first wife **Sarah Beecher** d. **1712 ae 32** — West Haven Green stone. Vitals in **FT-0127**; this mint adds marriage officiant + widow remarriage narrative.

## Full text

### Prindle 1906 (Joel³ row)

> m. Oct. 15, 1718, by Mr. Samuel Bishop, Jemima Benham, dau. of Nathan Benham, who was b. Sept. 21, 1700; d. Feb. 23, 1725-6. She m. (2) July 9, 1729, John Morgan.

### Sarah (Beecher) Benham stone (Jacobus via WikiTree Beecher-34)

> SARAH WIFE OF NATTHAN BENHAM DIED DECEMR 18 1712 AGED 32 YEARS

> Children of Sarah (Beecher) and Nathan Benham … Jemima b. 21 Sep 1700, New Haven; m. (1) 15 Oct 1718 Joel Prindle; m. (2) 9 Jan 1729 in Wallingford, John Morgan
