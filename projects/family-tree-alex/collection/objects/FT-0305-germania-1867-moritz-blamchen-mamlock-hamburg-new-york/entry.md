## Catalog note

**Earlier Mamlock landing on the same Hamburg→NYC corridor.** Steamer **Germania** (Master H. F. Schumper) arrived **27 May 1867** with **Moritz Mamlock**, age **37**, occupation **Merchant**, and **Blamchen Mamlock**, age **33**, **His wife** — both from **Germany**, cabin passengers. Rosa Mamlock and four children follow on **Allemannia** **9 Sep 1867** (FT-0260).
