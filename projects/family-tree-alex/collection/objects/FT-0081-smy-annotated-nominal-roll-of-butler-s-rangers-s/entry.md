## Full text (verbatim from page photo)

**Short, John Henry.** Private. Taken in Jan 1778, on the Susquehanna River near Tioga, by the Westmoreland Militia with sixteen men who were Rangers, which would lead one to suspect that he, too, was a Ranger.// E(52); E(75).
