## Full text

### Gazetteer notes (as published)

**INKERMAN** — A village in Mountain Township, Dundas County, Ontario 18 miles northwest of Morrisburg, 30 miles west of Cornwall, the county seat, and 5 miles southwest of Winchester, on the C P R, the nearest railway and banking point. It contains Methodist and Anglican churches and a public school. Stage daily to Iroquois. Pop 300. …from 1898-99 Eastern Ontario Gazetteer and Directory

**INKERMAN**, a post village in Dundas County, Ontario, on the C.P.R., 96 miles west of Montreal. The south branch of the Nation River runs through the village… It contains **2 Methodist churches**, 3 stores, four daily mails and telegraph office Pop. 300 …from Lovell’s 1906 Canada Gazetteer

### Church History (1983 local newspaper clipping, as quoted)

On August 15, **1883**, a cornerstone was laid for the new Methodist Church in the village of Inkerman, about 60 kilometers southeast of Ottawa.

One hundred years ago Inkerman was thriving. The population had risen to 400 and the **Wesleyan Methodist and the Methodist Episcopalians combined** to erect a new church. The laying of the cornerstone was an auspicious occasion.

The ceremony was under the jurisdiction of the Masons of Canada and the dignitaries, including the Lt. Gov. of Ontario John Beverley Robinson, the pastor Rev. John Stewart, members of the Masonic order and church trustees, were led to the site of the new church by the Winchester Brass Band. Also in attendance were James Gillespie, mason, John Toye, carpenter, and members of the congregation.

…a United Church since Union in 1925…

And the **red brick building** still stands proudly at the entrance to the village, a visible symbol of a bygone age…from a 1983 local newspaper clipping.
