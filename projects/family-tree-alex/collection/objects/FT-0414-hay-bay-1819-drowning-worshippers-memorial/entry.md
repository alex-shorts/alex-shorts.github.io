## Catalog note

**Hard** place memorial at Old Hay Bay: worshippers drowned **29 Aug 1819** on their way to the church; dedicated **27 Aug 1961**. Complements Hay Bay building/context objects (FT-0044–0047) with a named tragic beat for kids’ sourced hard history — not a personal Shorts death.

## Full text

```text
TO
the Glory of God
AND IN GRATEFUL MEMORY
OF THOSE WHO WERE DROWNED
ON THEIR WAY TO WORSHIP
IN THE OLD HAY BAY CHURCH
AUGUST 29, 1819
AND OTHER UNNAMED PIONEERS
BURIED HERE
DEDICATED AUGUST 27, 1961
```
