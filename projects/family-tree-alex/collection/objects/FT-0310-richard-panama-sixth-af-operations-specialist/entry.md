## What “Panama” meant for AAF personnel

Sixth Air Force (lineage: Panama Canal Air Force → Caribbean Air Force → Sixth AF) was headquartered at **Albrook Field**, Canal Zone. Primary wartime job: defend the **Panama Canal** (the Navy’s Atlantic–Pacific shortcut) and run **antisubmarine** patrols in the Caribbean / Gulf approaches when U-boats hit tanker traffic hard in **1942**. After German submarine pressure eased later in 1942, emphasis stayed on canal air defense and readiness. Campaign credit on the numbered force: Antisubmarine, American Theater.

## What “Operations Specialist” meant

AAF MOS lists include **791 Air Operations Specialist**. Period specialty language (Military Yearbook Project MOS digest): assists in administration of an AAF operations office — flight records and reports; operations orders; accident/damage reports; issue of flying clothing/equipment; dispatch support (routes, position logs, weather clearances, station-to-station flight traffic).
