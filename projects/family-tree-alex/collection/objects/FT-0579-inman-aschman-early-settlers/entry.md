## Full text

From *McPherson Weekly Republican*, Friday, 9 Jul 1920 (GenealogyTrails obit_A):

> Mrs. Hanna Aschman passed away at her home in Inman yesterday evening at five o'clock, from heart failure after an illness of ten days. She was 73 years. The deceased and her husband were **old and well known settlers in the Inman neighborhood, settling there over forty years ago** and **owning in the early days the land now occupied by the city of Inman**. They afterwards lived on a farm near Lake Inman for a number of years and of late years lived in Inman… The funeral services will be held at the home Thursday morning at ten o'clock and interment will be made at the **Inman Cemetery**.
