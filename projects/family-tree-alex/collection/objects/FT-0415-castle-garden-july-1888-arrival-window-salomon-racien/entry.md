## What Castle Garden was (NPS)

Castle Garden (Castle Clinton) was the **first official U.S. immigrant landing depot** (**3 Aug 1855 – 18 Apr 1890**). NPS: over **8 million** people entered through it; about **two of every three** U.S. immigrants in that period passed there. After federal control and the opening of Ellis Island, the building later became the New York Aquarium.

Source: [NPS Castle Clinton — History & Culture](https://www.nps.gov/cacl/learn/historyculture/index.htm)
