## Catalog note

**Primary open newspaper** for August Tuxhorn’s Nov 1884 farm fire / suicide — free Indiana reprint of the national wire (not the paywalled NYT page).

## Full text

WESTERN.

August Tuxhorn, a farmer near Me Pherson, Kan., was foiled in an attempt to kill his wife and four children. He then fired all the builnings on his place and blew out his brains. In the burned house was $4,000 In money.

*(OCR as held on Hoosier State Chronicles; “builnings” / “Me Pherson” / “In” capitalization are OCR artifacts. Remainder of the WESTERN column continues with unrelated Midwest items.)*
