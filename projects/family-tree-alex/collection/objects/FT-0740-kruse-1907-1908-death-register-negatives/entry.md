## Catalog note

Iowa 1904+ death registers often name **Father / Mother**. These open transcriptions were the best free chance to climb Henry × Marie. **Neither death is in the Allamakee 1907–1908 compilation nor the Houston County 1907 / 1908 registers**, even though New Albin undertakers appear in Houston (e.g. N. H. Thompson). Supports **C2745** **C2746**.
