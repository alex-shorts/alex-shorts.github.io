## Catalog note

Colonial Prindle parent + Joel×Jemima marriage/household extract from open Pane-Joyce (Jacobus *Families of Ancient New Haven* derivative).

## Full text

**Mary Brown (6774) & Joseph Prindle** — child entries (excerpt):

**18812. Samuel Prindle.** Born on 11 Nov 1693 in New Haven, CT. Baptized First Congregational Society, New Haven, 26 Nov 1693. Died 1767. m. 24 Apr 1718 Mary Smith. Children at New Haven: Esther b. 1 Feb 1718/9; Moses b. 4 Aug 1724; Joseph b. 17 Jul 1730.

**18813. Joel Prindle.** Born 28 Jan 1695/6 New Haven, CT. Baptized 15 Mar 1695/6. Died New Haven 23 Feb 1725/6. On **15 Oct 1718** m. **Jemima Benham**, dau. **Nathan Benham (10 Jul 1679–Jun 1757)** & **Sarah Beecher (ca 1679–18 Dec 1712)**, New Haven. Jemima b. **21 Sep 1700** New Haven; d. Cheshire **8 Aug 1752**. Children: William b. 2 Oct 1719; Sarah b. 9 Jul 1721 (m. Andrew Ives); Lois b. 21 Feb 1724; Joel b. 16 Jan 1725/6.

**18814. Hephzibah Prindle.** b. 15 May 1698 New Haven; d. 1796; m. 24 Jun 1724 Abiel Roberts.

**18815. Mary Prindle.** b. 1 Feb 1701/2 New Haven; d. ca 1733; m. 21 Dec 1726 John Bristol.
