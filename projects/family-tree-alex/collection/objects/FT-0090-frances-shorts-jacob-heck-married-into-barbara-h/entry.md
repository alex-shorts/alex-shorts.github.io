## Catalog note

**John/Rosannah generation — sister Frances:** one of the five Shorts orphans marries **Jacob Heck**, son of **Paul & Barbara Heck** (“Mother of Methodism in North America”). They later live in the Georgian stone **Heck House** on the St. Lawrence in Augusta Township.

## Full text

### Hay Bay — Shorts Family (Frances)

> Frances (b. 1774; d. 10 April 1844) was married in Montreal on **3 June 1792** to **Jacob Heck** (1769-1847). Jacob was a son of Paul and Barbara Heck (often called “the Mother of Methodism in North America”). For many years he worked as a shoemaker in Quebec City, but in **1807**, three years after his mother’s death, he moved to Augusta township and built the large Georgian rubble stone mansion on the shore of the St. Lawrence referred to as “**the Heck House**.” Frances and Jacob had five daughters, only one of whom married.

### Blue Church burying ground (Augusta) — stones

F. J. French, “Gleanings from the Blue Church Burying Ground”:

> Frances, wife of Jacob Heck, d. Apr. 10, 1844, age 73 years.  
> Jacob Heck, d. Sept. 24, 1847, age 78 years.

(Same ground holds Barbara Heck’s memorial monument — FT-0091.)
