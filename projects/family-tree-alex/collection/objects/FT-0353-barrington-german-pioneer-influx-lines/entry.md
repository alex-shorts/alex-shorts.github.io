## Catalog note

**Secondary / local-historian.** Barrington’s own historians describe Yankee settlers joined by a **German farming influx** in the 1840s–1850s — the open prairie world into which the Hilgenfeld family moved after one year in New York state (FT-0097). Ambient only; no surname match on these pages.

## Full text

**Arnett C. Lines — Barrington Area Library, Local History Part 1:**

> Lambert Meiners came from Germany in 1849 and Bernhard H. Landwer and his brother, Lambert, and son, Gerhart H. arrived from Germany to settle on Sec. 1. …

> Folks came from Europe many around here from Germany, by sailing ship, a journey which often took as much as six weeks. Some were on the water twelve weeks.

**Quintessential Barrington — South Barrington feature (local historian synthesis):**

> Soon, Yankees of English, Irish, and Scottish heritage were joined by Germans.
