## Catalog note

**Adoption narrative** (compiled, not a court minute): Charlotte appears in **Pioneer Life on the Bay of Quinte** p. 131 and Arthur Aylsworth **1887** p. 89 as daughter of David Bowerman, but the Bowerman compiler states she was **adopted** and omits her from David's biological descent list. **Biological mother remains unknown** — do not invent (**C1194 / C1246**).

## Full text

"Charlotte Bowerman" – is here given in the "Elsworth Family" as a dau. of David and Catherine; but as Charlotte was an adopted daughter, her name is omitted altogether from the descendants of David Bowerman.

As Charlotte was an adopted dau. of David and Catherine, and as no mention of her name is made in the family records of the brothers of David her name is omitted from the descendants of David Bowerman.

## Notes

- PLBQ p. 131 lists Charlotte among David × Catherine children **without** adoption caveat — treat as subscription genealogy ceiling.
- Williams genealogy PDF lists Charlotte among eleven children with no adoption note — secondary to Bowerman appendix.
- No open guardianship or adoption register found (**C1246** negative).
