## Catalog note

Official Continental **rank ladder** for Hugh Maxwell of Massachusetts. Highest wartime rank: **Lieutenant Colonel** (not full Colonel — “Col. Hugh” is courtesy). Heitman also dates the Bunker Hill wound as **Captain**, 17 Jun 1775.

Washington relation in the national papers: **one** indexed hit. Col. William Prescott wrote **to** Washington, 9 Dec 1775, about discharging Lt. James Russell, “lieutenant of Capt. Hugh Maxwell’s company in Prescott’s Massachusetts regiment.” Chain of command, not correspondence between the two men.

## Full text

### Heitman, *Historical Register* (1914), p. 384 (OCR)

> Maxwell, Hugh (Mass). Lieutenant of a Company of Minute Men at Lexington, 19th April, 1775; Captain of Presscott’s Massachusetts Regiment, 10th May to December, 1775; wounded at Bunker Hill, 17th June, 1775; Captain 7th Continental Infantry, 1st January to 31st December, 1776; Captain 2d Massachusetts, 1st January, 1777; Major 15th Massachusetts, 7th July, 1777; transferred to 2d Massachusetts, 1st July, 1779; Lieutenant-Colonel, 8th Massachusetts, 1st August, 1782; transferred to 3d Massachusetts, 12th June, 1783, and served to 3d November, 1783. (Died 14th October, 1799.)

Regiment officer lists in the same volume:

- **15th Mass.** — Major Hugh Maxwell, 7 Jul 1777 to 1 Jul 1779 (Col. Timothy Bigelow).
- **2d Mass.** — Major Hugh Maxwell, 1 Jul 1779 to 1 Aug 1782 (Col. John Bailey).
- **8th Mass.** — Lieutenant-Colonel Hugh Maxwell, 1 Aug 1782 to 12 Jun 1783 (Col. Michael Jackson).
- **3d Mass.** — transferred 12 Jun 1783 (the STCC “Third Massachusetts” end-state).

Cincinnati / 8th Mass. roster line in the same book: “Maxwell, Hugh, Lieutenant-Colonel, 8th.”

### Founders Online — Prescott to Washington, 9 Dec 1775

Sewall’s Point. Prescott consents to discharge Lt. James Russell, whose family affairs require him at home.

Editor’s note: “James Russell (d. 1801), lieutenant of Capt. Hugh Maxwell’s company in Prescott’s Massachusetts regiment, was discharged on this date.”

## Notes

- Prefer Heitman over memoir for Continental dates. Memoir “Major in **1779**” = transfer to 2d Mass under the Heath years, not the first major’s commission (**7 Jul 1777**, 15th Mass). Memoir Lt. Col. “rank **12 Oct 1782**” vs Heitman **1 Aug 1782**.
- White’s captaincy **26 May 1775** vs Heitman **10 May 1775** — same spring, Heitman for the register.
- Irish/Drake “Lieutenant at Bunker Hill” is weaker than Heitman/White (Captain, wounded).
- No Founders hit of Washington writing *to* Maxwell. Friendship letter is Heath → Maxwell, 13 Feb 1799 (**FT-0320**).
- FIW ensign **31 Mar 1759** is colonial (Pownall), not in Heitman — stays FT-0320 / FT-0327.

### Supports

- Supports: Minute-man Lt 19 Apr 1775; Capt Prescott May–Dec 1775; Bunker Hill wound as Captain; Capt 7th Continental 1776; Capt 2d Mass Jan 1777; Major 15th Mass 7 Jul 1777; Major 2d Mass from 1 Jul 1779; Lt Col 8th Mass 1 Aug 1782; 3d Mass from 12 Jun 1783 to 3 Nov 1783; company named in a letter to Washington Dec 1775
- Does not support: Full Colonel as Continental rank; Washington–Maxwell private correspondence; “friendship of Washington”
