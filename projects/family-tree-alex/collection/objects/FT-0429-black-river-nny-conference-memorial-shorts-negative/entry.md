## Catalog note

**Negative evidence object:** the standard open **Black River + Northern New York Conference Memorial** (Second Series, 1881) contains **no** minister sketch for any **Shorts** or **Dorland** surname. Useful for Dorland’s still-thin charge list: compiled sources say “Minister” (FT-0067) and he died Marcy NY (FT-0065/FT-0068 place context), but this memorial series does not carry him or a namesake predecessor.

> BLACK RIVER AND NORTHERN NEW YORK CONFERENCE MEMORIAL. SECOND SERIES.  
> Containing Sketches of the Life and Character of the Deceased Members of the above Conferences, not included in the former work. Brought down to the Present Time, **1880**.  
> By Rev. P. Douglass Gorrie. Watertown, N. Y.: Charles E. Holbrook, Printer. **1881.**

### Conference origins (excerpt)

> The Black River Annual Conference was organized in Watertown, N. Y., on the 1st day of September, **1836**, Bishop Beverly Waugh presiding…

> Resolutions adopted by the **Northern New York Conference of 1879**… requested continuation of memorial sketches for deceased members.
