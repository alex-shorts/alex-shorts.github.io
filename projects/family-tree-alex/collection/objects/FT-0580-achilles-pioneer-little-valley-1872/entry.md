## What the register means

McPherson Public Library (same language as FT-0098):

> As part of the observation of the **centennial of the county in 1970**, the McPherson County Historical Society… invited [persons] to submit names of their pioneer ancestors…

So **1872** is a descendant-reported settlement year for Little Valley — not a contemporaneous ledger entry from 1872.
