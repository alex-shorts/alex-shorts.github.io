## Catalog note

**J. A. Stonebraker** appears on the town’s 1910 reminiscence roll of residents who arrived **1875–1885** — same father whose home hosted **Urtha × Hugh** (**FT-0340**). Soft landscape for Hugh’s in-law cluster; does not invent Hugh occupation or parentage.

## Full text

**Henderson Times, Thursday 15 Dec 1910** (“Henderson of Yesterday”), via City of Henderson:

> …we have prepared a list of the oldest residents, who arrived between 1875 and 1885… They are: … Henry Maxwell, **J.A. Stonebraker**, R.S. and T.R. Stewart, …
