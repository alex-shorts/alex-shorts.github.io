## Catalog note

Death notice for **Mrs. Emily Anderson**, mother of **H. W. (Harry) Anderson**. Died **Monday night** (5 Nov 1923) near **10 o’clock** at **Shenandoah**; Harry and wife reached her before death. Names two other sons besides Harry: **E. U. / Ed** and **H. D. / Hugh**, of **Portland, Oregon**. Funeral expected **Saturday 2:30** at Silver City (short grave service). **“Obituary next week.”**

**H. D. Hugh** is the naming record for Hugh as Emily’s son. Portland 1923 is a west-move (or west-resident) note — do not merge to **Hugh Darwin** (× Maupin, San Jose) on this clip alone.

## Full text

**The Silver City Times, Thursday 8 Nov 1923, p.1** (Advantage OCR; punctuation lightly cleaned; original scan linked above):

> **MRS. EMILY ANDERSON**
>
> H. W. Anderson here received a phone message from Shenandoah Monday that his mother, Mrs. Emily Anderson was very low and could not last many hours. Harry and wife drove down, getting there before she passed away, which was Monday night near 10 o'clock.
>
> There are two other boys besides Harry—E. U. and H. D., Ed and Hugh, of Portland, Oregon. Ed is sick and can't come, but his wife is on the way. Hugh could not be reached, as he was enroute through Canada to the Twin cities at the time, but was expected to come on down here and may happen to drop in by Saturday, the day set for the funeral.
>
> The remains will be brought to Silver City, expecting that the funeral hour will be at 2:30 Saturday. A short service at the grave, and the casket will be opened. Obituary next week.
