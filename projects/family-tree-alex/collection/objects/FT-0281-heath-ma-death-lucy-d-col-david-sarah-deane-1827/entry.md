Heath death row for **Lucy** naming parents **Col. David** and **Sarah Deane** — supports soft mother **Sarah (Deane) Baldwin** for the Roxana sibling cluster (C1581).

> **Lucy**, d. David and-----, May 10, 1827. [inflamation on the brain, a. 20. **Lucy D., d. Col. David and Sarah Deane**, May 15, a. 20.]

- Lucy bp. **30 Jun 1794** (*d. David and w.*) vs death **1827 ae 20** — age tension noted in proposal C1581; death parent names still cite **Sarah Deane**.
