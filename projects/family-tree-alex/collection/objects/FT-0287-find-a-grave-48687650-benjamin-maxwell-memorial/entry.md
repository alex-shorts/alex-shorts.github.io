**Biography on memorial**

> Benjamin Maxwell, (1737-1829), served at Ticonderoga in Capt. Lawrence Kemp's company and in the Burgoyne campaign under Capt. Nahum Ward and Col. David Wells. He was born at Woburn, and died at Heath.

> Thursday, August 24, 1797 Paper: Greenfield Gazette (Greenfield, MA) … the dwelling house of Lieut Benjamin Maxwell, of Heath, was struck by lightning … no lives were lost …

> Monday, July 30, 1804 Paper: Greenfield Gazette … Benjamin Maxwell of Heath … "4 creatures" broke into his enclosure in Charlemont around July 1st …
