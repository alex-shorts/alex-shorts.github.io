## Full text

**III. David Bowerman**, b. 1754, d. 1828, m. Catherine Bartlett; set. Hallowell.

**Issue:** (1) John, (2) Benoni, (3) Levi, (4) Mary, (5) Cornelius, (6) Ichabod, (7) Sarah, (8) Elizabeth, (9) Lydia, (10) Phoebe, and **(11) Charlotte**.

*(Adjacent entries on p. 131 list sibling stems Jane, Hannah, Sarah, Jonathan Bowerman — FAN only.)*
