## Catalog note

Heath death index cluster for **Lt. William Buck** × **Sarah (Maxwell) Buck** and son **William Jr.** Supports C1540–C1541 (spouse/mother FAN) and C1547 (William death).

## Full text

From *Vital Records of Heath, Massachusetts, To the Year 1850* (deaths transcript, **BUCK** section):

> **William Jr.,** s. Lt. William and Sarah, Jan. 10, 1817. [William Jr., putrid fever, a. 21. William Jr., s. Lt. William and Sarah, a. 21. William Jr., a. 21 y. 3 m. 9 d.]

> **Sally,** w. William, Mar. 14, 1839 [dup. in later handwriting, **Sarah**, ch. Lt. Benjamin Maxwell and Anna (Winslow), Mar. 14, 1839, a. 76.]. [Sarah, a. 76. Sarah, in 67th y. Sarah M., a. 76.]

> **William,** h. Sally, Apr. 1, 1839. [h. Sarah, a. 84., in 84th y.]

## Notes

- Sarah death row names maiden parentage *Lt. Benjamin Maxwell* × *Anna (Winslow)* — see **FT-0284**.
- Does not soft-climb William Buck's own parents (C1546).
