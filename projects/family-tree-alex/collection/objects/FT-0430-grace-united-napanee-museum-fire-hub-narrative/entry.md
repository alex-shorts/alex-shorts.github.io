## Full text

### Museum of Lennox and Addington — Goblet object story (1975.532)

Goblet belonged to Grace United Church, which was originally named Grace Methodist Church. Grace Methodist Church was built on the grounds of the Napanee Academy. The church was built in **1842**.

The church went through several unfortunate natural disasters. In **1899** lightning struck the churches steeple causing significant damage. There was a major fire in the church **January 15th, 1943**, which cost **$35,000** to rebuild.

Throughout the years Grace Methodist/Grace United Church was a hub of activity. In **1912** there was a junior's boy's baseball team. Throughout the first half of the 20th century there is evidence that there were large Christmas pageants, and many adult Bible classes every week. There were also many weddings that took place at the church including Gertrude Orser and Mac Storey in April **1948**, as well as Elizabeth Tulloch and James Harrington in June of **1966**.
