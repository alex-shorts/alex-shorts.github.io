## Full text

**Nathan Benham** — b. **10 July 1679** (John Benham × Mercy Smith). d. **June 1757** Cheshire church records. m. (1) **Sarah**, d/o John & Elizabeth **Beecher** — d. **18 Dec 1712** ae 32 West Haven. m. (2) Mary ~1713.

**Children by Sarah:**

1. **Jemima** — b. **21 Sep 1700** NH; m. (1) **15 Oct 1718** Joel **Prindle**; m. (2) **9 Jan 1729** John Morgan.
2. **Mercy** — b. **6 Aug 1703**; d. **18 Apr 1766** ae 63; m. Caleb Hull.
3. **Samuel** — b. **29 Jul 1706**; d. young.
4. **Lydia** — m. Caleb Atwater.
5. **Sarah** — b. c. **1712**; d. **13 Nov 1751** ae 50; m. Henry Hotchkiss.
