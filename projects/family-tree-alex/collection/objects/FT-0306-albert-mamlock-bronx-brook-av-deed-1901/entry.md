## Catalog note

**Albert Mamlock in NYC property records.** The *Real Estate Record and Builders' Guide* (**26 Oct 1901**, vol. 68 no. 1754) lists a Bronx conveyance dated **18 Oct 1901**:

> **Nathan, Marcus to Albert Mamlock.** Brook av, e s, 125 n 171st st, 25×100.11. **Oct 18, 1901.** $1,000

Albert was **~43** (b. Germany per Allemannia 1867 age 7) and **~34 years** after landing with mother Rosa (FT-0260). Same index volume also records an Albert Mamlock **subordination agreement** on Westchester av property (**11 Jun 1908**) — not excerpted here.
