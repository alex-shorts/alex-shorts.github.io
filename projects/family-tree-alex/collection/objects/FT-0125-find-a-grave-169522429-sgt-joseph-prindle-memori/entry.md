## Catalog note

Open FAG biographical memorial for **Joseph Prindle Sr.** (Serjeant stone).

## Full text

**Vitals:** b. **11 Jun 1663**; d. **18 Mar 1738** (aged 74). Burial West Haven Green Cemetery.

**Spouse:** Mary Brown — m. **19 Aug 1686** New Haven.

**Children (memorial):** Joanna (1687–1688); John (1691–1723); Samuel (1693–1767); **Joel (1695–1796) m. Jemima Benham**; Hepzibah (1698–1796); Mary (1701–1733); Joseph Jr. (1703–1771).

**Bio beats:** 1697/8 — 88 acres on Half Way Hill (Prindle Hill / Scotland, 173 years in family). Episcopal Church founding role. **12 Dec 1728 will** — wife life estate; legacies to children/grandchildren. d. **18 Mar 1737/8**; estate **£808**; stone **Serjeant**.

## Gravesite Details

> The stone was worn in 1934 when Hale Cemetery Inscriptions recorded "Serjeant Joseph Prindle, died Mar 15". The stone has continued to deteriorate.
