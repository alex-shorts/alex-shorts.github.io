## Catalog note

London Port embarkation for **Increase** (Robert Lea), **April 1635**. **William Buck**, plowwright, age **50**, and **Roger Buck**, age **18** — identity spine for Cambridge Buck immigrant father/son.

## Full text

> THEIS pties hereafter expressed are to be transported to New-England imbarqued in ye Increase ROBERT LEA Mr having taken the oathes of Allegeance & Supremacie : As also being conformable to the Governmt & discipline of the Church of England…

> Plowrite. Wilht BUCK 50  
> ROGER BUCK - 18

(OCR: *Wilht* = William.)
