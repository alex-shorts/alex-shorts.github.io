## Catalog note

Primary open conference minutes placing **Andrew Prindle** among “superannuated or worn-out preachers” in **1829**, with the next Canada Conference set at **Ancaster, Bowman’s chapel** — institutional tie between Prindle’s later Ancaster retirement (FT-0032 / FT-0049) and the ME conference apparatus.

## Full text

### Quest. 8 — Superannuated or worn-out preachers (1829)

> Quest. 8. Who are the superannuated or worn-out preachers?  
> Thomas Whitehead, William Brown, Peter Jones, J. B. Smith, James Jackson, Andrew Prindle, W. Chamberlaine — 7.

### Niagara District numbers (context — Ancaster circuit active)

Minutes list Niagara District societies including **Ancaster 515** (whites), alongside York, Young-street & Whitby, Niagara and Fort George, Dumfries, etc.

### Quest. 14 — Stationing (excerpt)

> Niagara Dist. J. Ryerson, P. Elder.  
> …  
> Ancaster, Wm. Slayter, Egerton Ryerson.  
> …

(Prindle is on the worn-out list this year, not in the active stationing block.)

### Quest. 15 — Next Conference

> Quest. 15. Where and when shall our next Conference be held?  
> At Ancaster, Bowman’s chapel, district of Gore, Upper Canada, August 26, 1829.
