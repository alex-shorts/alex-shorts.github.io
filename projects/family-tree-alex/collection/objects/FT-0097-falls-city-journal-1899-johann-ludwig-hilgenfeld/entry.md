## Catalog note

**Migration + family-name story.** Sophia Maria Hilgenfeld Gatz’s father dies in Nebraska; the paper names her as **Sophia Gatz** and sketches the family’s path from Prussia to the Midwest.

## Full text

*The Falls City Journal*, Nov 10, 1899 (WikiTree Hilgenfeld-6 transcript):

> Johann Ludwig Hilgenfeld died at the home of his son Ludwig Hilgenfeld in this city last Saturday morning at the age of 92 years, and six months. For almost a century Mr. Hilgenfeld had been a pilgrim on the path of life, but on last Saturday the end was reached and he was gathered to his fathers. Father Johann Ludwig Hilgenfeld was born on April 22, 1807 at Wreben, Altmark, Prussia and died of old age on November 4, 1899 in Falls City, Nebraska at the home of his son Ludwig Hilgenfeld with whom he had lived for the last 23 years. In the year **1855**, he and his family came to America. The remained in **New York state for one year**, when they removed to **Barrington, Cook County, Illinois**, where they lived until **1868** when they removed to **Falls City, Nebraska**. Dorothea, the wife of Mr. Hilgenfeld died about eight years after settling here. They were married 49 years and their married life was very happy. Since the death of his wife Mr. Hilgenfeld lived at the home of his son where he quietly passed away on the above date at the age of 92 years, six months and 12 days. Father Hilgenfeld embraced religion about 24 years ago through the aid of J. F. Schneiber and remained true to God and the church to the end. Mr. Hilgenfeld leaves one son (Ludwig) and one daughter (**Sophia Gatz**), 17 grandchildren and 47 great-grandchildren. Mr. Hilgenfeld could read readily without spectacles to the last and attended church before his death. "The Lord gives to all the everlasting life and a happy reunion in heaven." The bereaved family have the sympathy of many friends in their bereavement. The funeral services were held from the residence of Ludwig Hilgenfeld on North Stone Street, Monday morning and was very largely attended by friends and family.

3. Widowhood ~8 years after settling in Nebraska; long residence with son Ludwig.
4. Surviving children named: Ludwig and **Sophia Gatz** — our McPherson line’s mother.
