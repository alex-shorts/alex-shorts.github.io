## What local historians say about that arrival window

**Paul R. Spitzzeri, Homestead Museum blog (10 Nov 2016)** — Firestone Tire groundbreaking **16 Dec 1927** on a former **bean field** at Alameda & Firestone in present South Gate; plant completed **Jun 1928** to make smaller tires for the West Coast market (larger tires stayed in Akron). Architects Curlett & Beelman; expanded soon after opening and again by WWII toward ~**1 million sq ft**. Neighboring rubber corridor: Goodyear on Central; **Samson Tire 1929** (later Citadel). LA’s car culture made the basin a huge tire market.

**City of South Gate — History:** incorporation **20 Jan 1923** (pop. then ~2,500); “boom years” after; Firestone’s **first tire 15 Jun 1928**; early industry also Maas Chemical (1922), later GM assembly **1936**.

**LA County Library — South Gate Local History:** from Cudahy Ranch → South Gate Gardens sales **1917** → city **1923** (pop. 500 at incorporation in that account); industry marketed from the start; Depression financial trouble + **1933** quake (see FT-0373).
