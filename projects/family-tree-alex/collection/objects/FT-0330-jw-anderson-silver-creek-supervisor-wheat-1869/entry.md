## Full text

**Board of supervisors — 4 January 1869** (*Notes on Pottawattamie County*, GenealogyTrails compilation):

> January 4th, 1869, the board of supervisors convened in the new courthouse. The old members of the board were, Garner, Kapper, Smith, Strong, and Whipple; and the new members were: J. L. Felter, from James; John S. Goss, from Rockford; **J. W. Anderson, from Silver Creek**; C. G. McIntosh, from Crescent; Jesse Wright, from Boomer; and Josiah True, from Knox. Mr. Felter was elected chairman.

**Crop report — same compiled narrative (1869 season):**

> The farmers of the county were generally rewarded with good crops. … Of wheat, **J. W. Anderson, of Silver Creek township, raised 1,500 bushels on seventy five acres**; R. H. Woodmancy, of Macedonia, 480 bushels on twenty acres; and J. S. Goss, of Rockford, 450 bushels on thirty acres.
