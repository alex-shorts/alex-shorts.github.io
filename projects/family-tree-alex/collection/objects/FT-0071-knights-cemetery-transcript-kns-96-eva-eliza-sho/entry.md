## Catalog note

Primary open stone transcript for Eva at Knight’s Cemetery: Camden birth, death at **M.E. Parsonage Inkerman**, and **husband and son** survivors. Cemetery overviews captured; individual stone photo still absent on open web.

## Full text

### Cemetery intro (transcript header)

Knight's Cemetery, Inkerman  
The Methodist Episcopal Church was founded in Inkerman in **1855** and the Wesleyan Methodist in **1882**.  
Transcribed by Lynne and Mahlon Cook.

### KNS 96 — SHORTS

*(broke off base & leaning against #97)*

> In Memory of Eva Eliza wife of Rev. Alex. Shorts, born in township of Camden? Oct 3, 1849, departed this life at home, M. E. Parsonage Inkerman Aug. 9, 1876 aged 35 y'rs 9 m's  
> ?Grieving husband and son  
> To morn her loss on earth  
> But to rejoice in her rich  
> Experience of dying grace
