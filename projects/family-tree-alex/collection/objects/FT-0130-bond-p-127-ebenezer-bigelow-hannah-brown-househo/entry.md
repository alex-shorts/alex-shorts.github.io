## Full text

> (III.) EBENEZER BIGELOW, of Weston, m., May 24 1720, HANNAH BROWN, b. Oct. 5, 1699, dr. of Capt. Abraham and Mary (Hyde) Brown. She d. Nov. 3, 1775.

Children: Ebenezer b. 1720-1; Josiah bap. 1722 d. young; Ebenezer bap. 1724; Moses bap. 1730; Hopestill bap. 1731; Silence bap. 1732; Joshua bap. 1733; Abraham & Elijah bap. 1734; Josiah bap. 1736; Samuel bap. 1738; Benoni b. 1740.
