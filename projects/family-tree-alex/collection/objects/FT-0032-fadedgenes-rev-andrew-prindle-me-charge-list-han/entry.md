## Full text

### ANDREW PRINDLE – Methodist Episcopal Minister (fadedgenes)

Rev. Andrew Prindle was born in 1780 and died on January 15, 1855. He was received on trial in 1806 Methodist Episcopal.

The Rev. Andrew Prindle (1780-1855) was minister at Bowman Church Ancaster in 1809, and, after his superannuation, retired to Ancaster. He became so fat in middle life that a special buggy, with extra strong springs, had to be built for him, and a very wide chair was made for him. This unique chair it still preserved in Bowman Church as a prized relic of this popular preacher. He kept a small, leather bound book in which is recorded the marriage of Catharine House in 1843 to a Mr. Murray. Her monument is Kitty Murray Lane, which is named after her. Andrew Prindle and his wife lie buried on the east side of Bowman Church. In 1935, a cairn was built to hold his fallen tombstone, and a memorial service was conducted by the Rev. T. Albert Moore, a grandson of Mr. Moore of Milton who made the reinforced buggy…by Mrs. A. McCulloch

Charges:

1805 Niagara, 1806 Ottawa, 1807 Yonge St., 1808 Litchfield N.Y., 1809 Bowman Church Ancaster (Wentworth Cty), 1810 Niagara, 1811 Scarborough, 1812 Ancaster and Long Point, 1813-1814 Bay of Quinte, 1815-1816 Augusta, 1817 St. Lawrence, 1818-1819 Black River N.Y., 1820 Seneca N.Y., 1821-1822 Bloomfield N.Y., 1823 Genesee N.Y., 1824-1825 Batavia N.Y., 1826 Lewiston N.Y., 1827 St. Lawrence, 1828-1829 Ancaster C.W., 1830 Dumfries/Wellington Cty, 1831 Ancaster, 1832 Long Point, 1833 joined Wesleyan Conference, 1833 Galt, 1834-1839 Ancaster (retired), 1839 Oswegotchie District, 1840-1849 Hamilton (retired), 1851-1855 Glanford/Seneca superannuated
