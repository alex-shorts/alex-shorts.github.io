## Catalog note

**Mrs. Sarah E. Trask Buck** on faculty of **Platteville Normal** (Old Normal) as **Teacher of Reading**: hired **Sep 1876**; resigned end of fall term **Dec 1880**; **re-elected Sep 1880** for Reading. Overlaps **Duncan McGregor** becoming Normal **president 1 Jan 1879** — same McGregor who eulogized Erastus in **1901** (FT-0175).

## Full text

From the Platteville State Teachers College faculty chronicle:

> **1876—September.** Mrs. S. E. Buck was employed as Teacher of Reading.
>
> **1879—January 1st.** Duncan McGregor, President. Appointed January 1st to fill the vacancy caused by the resignation of Mr. Charlton, he was elected by the Board February 9.
>
> **1880—Changes in the faculty were:** Mrs. Buck resigned as teacher of reading at the close of the fall term (Dec. 1880). Miss Jennie S. Cooke was made "Special Teacher" in the Training Department instead of Assistant in the Grammar Grade as before.
>
> **1880—September.** … Mrs. Buck was re-elected teacher of Reading; …
