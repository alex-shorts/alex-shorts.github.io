## County school landscape (open)

From [McPherson County history — Appendix F](https://www.mcphersoncountyks.us/DocumentCenter/View/318):

> Rural public schools in the county numbered approximately **131** sometime during the **1930's**. After school consolidation, all of the public tax-financed schools are now in the incorporated cities. Only private schools remain in the rural areas.

Same document: **1900** average farm size **202 acres**; modern average **430 acres** — the consolidated-farm world Gloria left when the family drove west in **1942**.

## Say with kids

Great-grandma Gloria walked into a **one-room school** — one teacher, every age together — the way most McPherson County farm kids learned before the districts merged into town schools. Her obit is the personal proof; the county history shows it was normal, not exotic.
