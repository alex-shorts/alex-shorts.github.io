## Catalog note

Local news item: **Harry Anderson** sold his farm northeast of Silver City to **his brother Hugh** for **$23,800** (week of ~25 Sep 1913). Combined with Harry’s Silver City bio (**FT-0177**, son of **John W. Anderson** × **Emily E. Dupray**) and Hugh Dupree’s 1902 Silver City farm residence (**FT-0340**), this is the first free source that **names** Hugh as Harry’s brother.

## Full text

**The Silver City Times, Thursday 2 Oct 1913, p.1** (Advantage OCR; punctuation lightly cleaned; original scan linked above):

> Last week Harry Anderson sold his farm northeast of town to his brother Hugh for $23,800.
