## Catalog note

Primary **discipline narrative** for Charlotte: request under Friends care (**1803**), acceptance (**1803**), appointment to Monthly Meeting (**1804**), condemnation for marrying **Otho Nial** out of Unity (**1805**), acknowledgment **accepted** as **Charlotte Aylsworth** (**1806**). Adoptive mother **Catherine** (spelled Catarine) parallel request **1804**. No minute names biological parent.

## Full text

### West Lake Preparative Meeting of Women Friends (H-12-1)

**12th day of 7th month 1803:**

> Was handed into this Meeting by one of . – the Overseers a Request Signd **Charlotty Bowerman** desiring to come Under Friends care which is Referd to the Monthly Meeting for farther consideration

**7th day of 2nd month 1804:**

> **Charlotty Bowerman** and Sarah Carman are appointed to attend the Monthly Meeting

**6th month 1804** (Catherine Bartlett adoptive path):

> One quest came to this meeting by way of the overseers Sined **Catarine Bowerman** Signifien She hath adesire to Com under friends care wich was Red and is Recommed to the monthly Meeting

**12th of the 11th month 1805:**

> com to this meeting by way of the overseers Signed **Charlotte Elsworth** Condeming her misconduct for marring out of the unity of friends wich after a deliberation it is directed to the monthly meetings Consideration

### Adolphustown Monthly Meeting (O-2-1)

**15th of 9th month 1803:**

> The Womens meeting informed this that they had come to a conclusion to accept of **Charlottee Bowermans** Request if we concur with them therein … after consideration thereon we concur with them therein and accept her accordingly

**18th of 9th month 1806:**

> The Womens Meeting inform that they are united in accepting **Charlotte Aylsworths** acknowledgement this meeting concurs there with and accepts her a Member~
