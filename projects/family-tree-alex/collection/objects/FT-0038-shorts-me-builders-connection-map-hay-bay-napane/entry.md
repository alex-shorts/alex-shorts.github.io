### What they built — one line

**Augustus** helped **plant** Bay of Quinte ME (Hay Bay trustee + early ordination).  
**Alexander** **physically moved and rebuilt** a church across the international rift onto Grindstone.  
**Dorland** carried the **Rev** name into the next generation (Rochester / Oneida), while Alexander’s building was later saved (1930s) and memorialized in glass (FT-0037).
