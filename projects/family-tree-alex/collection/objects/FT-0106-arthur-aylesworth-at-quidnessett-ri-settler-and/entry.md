## Catalog note

**History lesson:** English Atlantic migration into Narragansett country; colonial Rhode Island town life; later **Rhode Island → Hudson → Canada** family movement (1887 “Emigration from Rhode Island” chapter + Bay of Quinte Loyalist vignettes in the same volume).  
**Family hinge:** Immigrant **Arthur Aylesworth (1)** (Probable in graph) settled **Quidnessett**; Eva Eliza Aylesworth Shorts (Confirmed) is the known Ontario descendant who married Rev. Alexander Shorts — connection point for classroom “how did our line get here?”

## 1887 book beats (open)

**First generation (pp. 36–37):** Arthur came to America before **29 Jul 1679**, married **Mary Brown** of Providence, settled Quidnesset; children include **Philip (2) b. 1692**; died between **7 Nov 1725** and **13 Sep 1726**.

**Register tradition:** born England ~**1656**, emigration often styled **~1681**.

**Emigration chapter (p. 47+):** tracks Aylsworth-name households still in RI towns in the **1774** census, then movement toward Hudson River counties, Vermont, and **Canada** (including Philip × Deborah Dyer Canada notes elsewhere in the volume).

**Loyalist-adjacent color (same volume):** Robert Perry / Hannah Washburn Bay of Quinte landing memory (French boats, tents near Bath, musketry exchange) — **Aylesworth-book lore**, not a claim that Eva’s exact ancestors are that Perry household. Use as period atmosphere for Upper Canada settlement lessons tied to the book that documents Arthur’s stem.

## Honest limits

- Arthur remains **Probable** / soft-ingest.
