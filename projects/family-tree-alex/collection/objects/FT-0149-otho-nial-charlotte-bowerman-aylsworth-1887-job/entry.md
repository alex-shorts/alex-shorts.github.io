## Catalog note

Compiled **family narrative** linking Charlotte Bowerman to the Aylesworth line that reaches **Eva Eliza Aylesworth Shorts** (Confirmed): Otho m. Charlotte **1805**; son **Jonathan Fairfield** b. **1812** m. Margaret Gilchrist → Eva. Same volume records grandfather **Job (3)** entering Canada **1788**, Ernesttown near Bath, and death in **1803 spotted fever** epidemic — period atmosphere for Otho's generation; not a claim that Charlotte witnessed Job's death.

## Full text

### Job (3) × Sarah Clark — Canada migration (1887 pp. 62–63)

He entered Canada in the scarce year, 1788, and settled in Ernesttown, near Bath, where he, his wife, and three children died during the prevalence of spotted fever in 1803.

He was a man of resolute character. Having a distressing felon he removed the affected finger himself with a chisel and mallet.

60. i Otho Nial (4) m. Charlotte Bowerman, 1805, and dwelt in Ernesttown.

### Otho Nial (4) × Charlotte Bowerman (1887 p. 89)

60. OTHO NIAL (4)- Job (3), Philip (2), Arthur (1) -married in 1805, Charlotte Bowerman, who was born in the year 1779, and who was the daughter of David Bowerman, and dwelt in Ernesttown, County of Lennox, Ontario. He died in 1821, and she in 1869.

Their children were five sons and four daughters:

176. V Jonathan Fairfield (5) b. 1812, m. Margaret Gilchrist; dw Richmond, Ont.
