## Catalog note

**Martha's Achilles ascent — migration beat.** Henry F. Achilles Sr. and Caroline raised Ida in Lee County, Iowa, then brought her to the **Inman community** as a child. Ida's 1951 obituary states the move; GEDBAS locks Iowa marriage and birth places.

## Migration narrative (sourced)

From [FT-0553](../FT-0553-hutchinson-news-ida-l-tuxhorn-1951/entry.md) — *Hutchinson News*, 10 Jan 1951:

> She was born in **Lee county, Ia.**, Aug. 29, 1866, **came to the Inman community with her parents when she was nine years old**.

Arithmetic: ~**1875** arrival in McPherson County if age nine.

From [GEDBAS Heinrich Henry Friedrich Achilles](https://gedbas.genealogy.net/person/show/1163945812):

- Born **25 May 1842**, **Barum, Salzgitter, Braunschweig, Germany**
- Married **Caroline D. Wischer**, **20 Jun 1865**, **Lee County, Iowa**
- Child **Ida Louise Achilles**, b. **29 Aug 1866**, Lee County

From [GEDBAS Ida Louise Achilles](https://gedbas.genealogy.net/person/show/1163945815):

- m. **Leo Lewis Tuxhorn**; d. **9 Jan 1951** Inman

## Story beats for family reading

1. **Germany → Iowa:** Henry and Caroline marry in **Lee County** right after the Civil War.
2. **Iowa → Kansas prairie:** They move Ida to the **Inman** neighborhood while she is still a child — the world Leo Tuxhorn will marry into in **1891**.
3. **Next generation:** Ida × Leo → **Martha Tuxhorn Gatz** → **Gloria** ([FT-0005](../FT-0005-conejo-mountain-funeral-home-gloria-martha-short/entry.md)).
