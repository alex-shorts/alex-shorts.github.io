## Catalog note

Sister-stone for **Rev Andrew Prindle** (Hannah Ellenor Shorts’s brother): inscription names **Eunice**, wife of **Henry Miller**, sister to the late **Rev. A. Prindle**, died **3 Mar 1856** in her 71st year. Hardens the Prindle preacher FAN for the Shorts ME line.

## Full text

```text
EUNICE,
Wife of
HENRY MILLER,
& Sister to the late
Rev. A. PRINDLE
DIED
March 3, 1856:
in the 71st year
```
