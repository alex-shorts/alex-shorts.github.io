## Catalog note

Memorial-card extract for **Hugh Dupray** (1815 – **3 Jul 1892**), Silver City Cemetery. Bio names **Thomas Dupray** as father and records marriage to **Mary Ann Chamberlin** **12 Oct 1841**, Portage Co., Ohio. Supports **C2541** father wire; does **not** name Hugh's mother.

## Full text

> Son of Thomas Dupray. Married to Mary Ann Chamberlin Oct. 12, 1841 in Portage Co. Ohio. Two children, Willis and Emily.
