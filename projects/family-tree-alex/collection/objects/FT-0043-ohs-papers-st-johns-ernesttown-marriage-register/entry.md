## Catalog note

Earliest open **church-register** hit placing Shorts orphans in the Adolphustown / Ernesttown neighborhood by **February 1792** — Philip and Katreen (Catharine) Shorts witness a Van Dusen–Brown marriage, consistent with the 1784 Adolphustown settlement narrative in FT-0030.

### Marriage Register of St. John's Church, Ernest Town, No. 2 — entry 5

5. David Brown, Adolphus; Catharine Van Dusen, do ..2 Feb., 1792.

Joseph Allison, **Philip Shorts**, **Katreen Shorts**.
