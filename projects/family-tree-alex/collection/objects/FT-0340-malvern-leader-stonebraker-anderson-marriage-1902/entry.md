## Catalog note

Social notice (beyond license-only **FT-0178** / return **FT-0192**) places the wedding at the home of **Mr. and Mrs. J. A. Stonebraker** near Henderson, names bride **Urtha** as their **youngest daughter**, and groom **Hugh Anderson**. Ceremony dated **Wednesday 28 May 1902** in the notice (Mills return **26 May** — date-variant known).

## Full text

**Malvern Leader, Thursday 5 Jun 1902, p.2** (Advantage OCR; punctuation lightly cleaned; original scan linked above):

> **Stonebraker-Anderson.**
>
> We are pleased to chronicle an important social event which occurred at the home of Mr. and Mrs. J. A. Stonebraker near Henderson, Wednesday the twenty-eighth of May, it being the marriage of their youngest daughter, Miss Urtha, to Mr. Hugh Anderson of Silver City.
>
> Promptly at two o'clock Miss Stella Powell of Red Oak began the beautiful wedding march from La Marseillaise and the bride and groom unattended took their place in the parlor before a screen of smilax where an impressive ceremony was solemnized by the Rev. N. C. McCrea of Carson.
>
> The bride's gown was a dainty creation of French mull with trimmings of medallion lace. The bridal veil was held in place by an amethyst pin. She carried a shower bouquet of bride's roses.
>
> After the usual congratulations had been extended to the happy couple, Mr. and Mrs. Anderson led the way to the dining room where a delicious buffet luncheon was served. Green and white made the pretty combination of color which was so effective in the decoration of this room.
>
> Mrs. F. M. Powell of Glenwood, an aunt of the bride, presided over the dining table assisted by a number of the bride's girl friends.
>
> Mr. and Mrs. Anderson were the recipients of numerous and elegant gifts showing the high esteem in which they are held among their friends. The bride is so well known that an introduction to our readers is unnecessary. Mr. Anderson though somewhat of a stranger in this vicinity is a young man of excellent qualities and has a host of friends in the neighborhood in which he lives. Mr. and Mrs. Anderson begin housekeeping on their farm near Silver City and will be at home to their many friends after July first.
>
> Following is a list of the guests: Mr. and Mrs. F. M. Powell, Glenwood; Rev. and Mrs. S. M. Perkins, Villisca; Mr. and Mrs. E. N. Anderson, H. N. Anderson and Miss Margarette McKinley, Silver City; Rev. McCrea, Carson; [remainder of guest list badly OCR'd].
