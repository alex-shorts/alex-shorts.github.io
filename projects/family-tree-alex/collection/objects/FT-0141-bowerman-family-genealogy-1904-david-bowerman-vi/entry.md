### David Bowerman (VI) — p. 16 summary

- **b. 1754**  
- **d. Apr 1828**, Prince Edward County, aged 74  
- **m. Catherine Bartlett**, Dutchess County, New York (~1775)  
- **Parents (compiled):** Ichabod Bowerman × Lydia Mott (**C1195** Possible)

**Children (biological, compiled):** John · Benoni · Levi · Mary · Cornelius · Ichabod · Sarah · Elizabeth · Lydia · Phebe — plus **Charlotte (adopted)**.

### Aylesworth / Elsworth appendix (Charlotte)

> Otho Nial, (d. 1821), mar. 1805, Charlotte Bowerman (b. 16th Oct. 1779; d. 27th Mar. 1869).

> As **Charlotte was an adopted dau. of David and Catherine**, and as no mention of her name is made in the family records of the brothers of David her name is omitted from the descendants of David Bowerman.
