## Catalog note

**California Shorts vertical — the meet-cute.** Gloria’s obituary says they met at the **Palladium in Hollywood** and danced to **Woody Herman’s band**, then married **22 Oct 1950**.

## Family text (FT-0005)

> She met Richard Shorts at the **Palladium in Hollywood**, where they danced to **Woody Hermans band**. They married October 22, 1950 and were devoted to each other for 59 years…

## What the Palladium was

Hollywood Palladium (6215 Sunset Blvd): Streamline Moderne dance hall opened **31 Oct 1940** (Tommy Dorsey / Frank Sinatra opening night). ~11,200 sq ft dance floor; capacity into the thousands. Built where the original Paramount lot stood; NRHP 2016.

## Woody Herman at the Palladium

Herman’s orchestra is documented live at the Hollywood Palladium in the early 1940s (commercial releases titled *At the Hollywood Palladium 1942–1944*; sessions cited for Aug 1942 and Nov 1944). Herman’s **First Herd** (mid-1940s) was one of the era’s hottest big bands — the sound Gloria’s generation danced to.

**Honest limit:** Obituary does not give the year of the dance. Gloria finished Bellflower High after the **1942** Kansas→CA move; marriage is **1950**. Herman played the Palladium across the 1940s into later Herds — treat “Woody Herman’s band” as family memory + documented Palladium association, not a pinned calendar night.
