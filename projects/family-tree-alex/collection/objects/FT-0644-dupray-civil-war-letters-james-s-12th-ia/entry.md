## Catalog note

**Primary-adjacent / archive finding aid.** UW Civil War Letters Collection describes **Ruth (Dupray) Morgan papers**: letters **Oct 1861 – Apr 1863** to Ruth in **Dubuque County, Iowa** from **William** and **James S. Dupray**. **James Dupray** served **Iowa Volunteers, 12th Regiment**. Upon return **August 1865** he found **wife deceased** and **children sent to live elsewhere** — matches **Caroline M. Dupray** Bible death **22 Feb 1865** (**FT-0532**) and Chamberlin sister line (**FT-0534** **FT-0637**).

## Full text

> **Ruth (Dupray) Morgan letters, 1861-1865.** Civil War letters written home to Iowa between October 27, 1861 and finished April 15, 1863. Four of the letters are written to his sister **Ruth (Dupray) Morgan** in **Dubuque County, Iowa** from family members **William and James S. Dupray**. **James Dupray** was a soldier in the **Iowa Volunteers, 12th Regiment**. Upon returning home in **August of 1865**, he finds his **wife deceased** and **children sent to live elsewhere**.
