## Catalog note

FAG memorial for **Mary (Walker Brown) Prindle**.

## Full text

**Vitals:** b. **2 May 1664**; d. **10 Dec 1753** (aged 89). Burial West Haven Green Cemetery.

**Parents:** John Brown × Mary Walker.

**Spouse:** Sgt. Joseph Prindle — m. **19 Aug 1686**.

**Children:** Joanna; John; Samuel; **Joel m. Jemima Benham**; Hepzibah; Mary; Joseph Jr.

**Bio:** Prindle Hill household; Episcopal participation; 1728 will life estate; 1753 death legacies via Joseph Jr.

## Gravesite Details

> Here lyes Buried ye Body of Mrs Mary Prindle wife to Sergnt Joseph Prindle of new Haven, Died Decembr ye 10th 1753 my 90th
