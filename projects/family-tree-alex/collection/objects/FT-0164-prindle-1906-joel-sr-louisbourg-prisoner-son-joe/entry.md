## Catalog note

**New colonial color** on the generation between Joseph² and UEL Joel⁴: eldest son **William⁴ died prisoner at Louisbourg (4 Jul 1747)**; widow Jemima remarried John Morgan; Joel⁴ of **Danbury** cites "father Joel and grandfather Joseph" in **1755** deed — compiler tags **"Revolu. soldier"** (Patriot service — contrasts later UEL Joel⁴ Loyalist arc in **FT-0144**; treat as same-name generation confusion risk).

## Full text

> iv. Joel³, b. Jan. 28, 1695-6; m. Oct. 15, 1718, by Mr. Samuel Bishop, Jemima Benham, dau. of Nathan Benham, who was b. Sept. 21, 1700; d. Feb. 23, 1725-6. She m. (2) July 9, 1729, John Morgan.
>
> Children:
> 1. William⁴, b. Oct. 2, 1719; d. while a prisoner at Louis- burg, July 4, 1747. unm.
> 2. Sarah⁴, b. 1721; m. Oct. 2, 1744, Andrew Ives; rem. to Wallingford, Conn.
> 3. Lois⁴, b. , 1724; m. Bristol; rem. to Wallingford, Conn.
> 4. Joel⁴, b. Jan. 16, 1725-6; m. and had a son. … In a deed given by Joel⁴, of Danbury, in 1755, he mentions his "father Joel and grandfather Joseph." Revolu. soldier.
