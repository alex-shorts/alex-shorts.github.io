Ventura County Star (CA) — October 28, 2009 (FAG memorial 65483876 bio transcript, contributor S. McCoskey, May 2021):

Richard Shorts passed away on Oct. 23, one day after his 59th wedding anniversary. He was born in Rochester, New York, July 29, 1922, the first son of Earl Stanley and Annabelle Lucretia Shorts. The family moved to Southern California in 1929. Richard graduated from South Gate High School in 1941, attended Compton Junior College and when the Shorts family moved to South Pasadena, he later went to Pasadena City College. During WWII, Richard joined the U.S. Army Air Corps. He was assigned to Victorville, Montana and finally Panama, where he kept flight records as an Operations Specialist. After being discharged in 1945, Richard returned to Pasadena City College, and helped his father at his Highland Park shoe store. Richard met his future wife, Gloria Gatz at the Hollywood Palladium when Woody Herman's band was featured. The couple married in Oct. of 1950 and had three children. Richard became a fireman, first in Arcadia, then Huntington Park and finally in the City of Burbank where he was to become a Captain. In 1965, Richard moved his family to Thousand Oaks. The family enjoyed summer vacations at their beach house on Silver Strand beach in Oxnard. Richard had a love for life. He was a wonderful and devoted husband to Gloria, and they shared a long and beautiful life together. He treasured and prioritized his family and built a strong and loving foundation for his children and grandchildren. He was a man of humor and fun, always having a joke to tell at any given moment. Richard also had a love for music, old cars, sports, the beach, and travel. As a grandfather, he was known as "Papa" and was a big part of his grandchildren's lives. He never wanted to miss their sporting events, even if it meant long road trips - he was their biggest fan. He never missed an opportunity to encourage and support those around him. Our hearts ache for his passing and we will miss him so much, but we rejoice that he is at peace in the presence of the Lord. Richard was predeceased by his daughter Carla Shorts. He is survived by his wife Gloria Shorts, his son Bill & daughter-in-law Heidi Shorts, his daughter Gloria (Gigi) Kilmer, his brother Don Allen Shorts, and his five grandchildren: Sam, Alex, & Nick Shorts and Bryce & Shelby Kilmer. A service of remembrance will be held at 2 p.m. Friday, Oct 30, in the Chapel of the Islands at Conejo Mountain Memorial Park in Camarillo. Interment will immediately follow. Arrangements are under the direction of Conejo Mountain Funeral Home, 2052 Howard Rd., Camarillo; phone 482-1959.

Headstone (Conejo Mountain): CPL U.S. ARMY AIR FORCES WORLD WAR II.
