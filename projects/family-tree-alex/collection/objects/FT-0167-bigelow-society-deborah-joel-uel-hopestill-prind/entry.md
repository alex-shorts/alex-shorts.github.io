## Full text

### Bigelow Society

> Debra Bigelow … married (about 1740's) Joel Pringle (Prindle?), born in New Haven, CT 1725 and moved to Danbury at a young age. He did eventually have land in Skenesborough (Whitehall), NY. He and his children came to the Kingston area as United empire Loyalists. Debra died age 65 years, 8 mo., 8 da., and is buried in the Loyalist cemetery at Adolphustown, Ontario.

> Hopestill … res Danbury, CT; Tyringham, MA; and Whitehall, NY … Hopestill's wife's sister, Eunice, married William Prindle…. Debra Bigelow, (who would be sister of Hopestill) married Joel Prindle.
