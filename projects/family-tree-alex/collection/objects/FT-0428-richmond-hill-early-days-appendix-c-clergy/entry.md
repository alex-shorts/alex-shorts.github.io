## Catalog note

Open **Appendix C** compendium placing **Rev Andrew Prindle** on the **Yonge Street circuit** in **1807** and again in **1811** — the Richmond Hill / Yonge Street world where Hannah’s brother rode before Bowman Ancaster (FT-0032 / FT-0076). Documents how Yonge Street “circuit riders spread the word … from 1803, meeting in members' homes or the open air” until Richmond Hill built its own Wesleyan chapel in 1846.

## Full text

### Framing — Yonge Street circuit riders (Appendix C intro)

> The Yonge Street circuit riders spread the word of Methodism to the community from 1803, meeting in members' homes or the open air until the Presbyterians offered to share their accommodation in the 1820s.

> Although the officially recognized church of Upper Canada was the Church of England, religious life in Richmond Hill revolved around the Methodists and Presbyterians for most of the first half of the 19th century.
