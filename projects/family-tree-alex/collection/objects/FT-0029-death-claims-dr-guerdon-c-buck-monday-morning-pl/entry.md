## Full text (substantial excerpt from FAG clip)

**Death Claims Dr. Guerdon C. Buck Monday Morning**  
**Well Known Physician and Surgeon Will be Buried Thursday.**  
*(newspaper date line Wednesday, June 22, 19—; year cut on clip edge; death Monday before burial Thursday; FAG death 20 Jun 1932)*

Dr. Guerdon C. Buck, well known physician and surgeon of this city, died Monday morning at his home, **460 Ellen Street**. He was born in Platteville **October 17, 1873**, the only son of the late **Dr. Erastus J. Buck** and wife **Mrs. Sarah Buck**. He attended Platteville Normal, the State University, and **Cornell College of New York**, and interned at **Bellevue Hospital**, New York City, for four years. He practiced medicine and surgery about thirty years, twenty-five of them in Platteville, and maintained a hospital here. He served many years on the Platteville school board.

Affiliations include Melody Lodge A.F. & A.M. of Platteville; Washington Chapter No. 2, R.A.M.; Platteville Commandery No. 35; Wisconsin Consistory and Tripoli Temple of Milwaukee; State Medical Society of Wisconsin; Grant County Medical Society (three terms as president); and the American Medical Society of Chicago.

Surviving are his widow, **Mrs. Hilda Moore Buck**; his mother, **Mrs. Sarah Buck** of Milwaukee; and six sisters: **Miss Gertrude** of Milwaukee; **Mrs. Arthur Nehls** of Buffalo, New York; **Mrs. Winifred Muckler** of Missoula, Montana; **Mrs. Ralph Saxton** of Seattle, Washington; **Miss Berenice** of Hammond, Indiana; and **Mrs. F. H. Hallett** of Chamberlain, South Dakota.

Funeral at the home, 460 Ellen St.; burial **Greenwood**. Pall bearers: Drs. Cunningham, Schuldt, C. Andrew, Wheeler, Soles, and Donnell. Clergy: Revs. Merritt, Doering, and Dixon.
