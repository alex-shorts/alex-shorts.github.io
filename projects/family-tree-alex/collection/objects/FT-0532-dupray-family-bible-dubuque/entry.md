## Catalog note

**Dupray Family Bible** family-record page (published **1854**). Names **James S. Dupray** b. **27 Sep 1816** Franklin, Venango Co. PA; **Caroline M. Chamberlain** b. **11 Jun 1820** Sheffield, Berkshire Co. MA; marriage **24 May 1842** Bavaria, Portage Co. OH. Corroborates Mary Ann FAG sibling note (**Caroline M. Chamberlin** × **James Story Dupray**). Does **not** name Hugh Dupray or John W. Anderson.

## Full text

**Family Record — Births**

> James S. Dupray was born in Franklin, Vernago Co, Pa Sept 27 1816  
> Caroline M Chamberlain was born in Sheffield Berkshire Co Mass June 11th 1820  
> Celia Augusta was born in Streetsboro Portage Co Ohio Aug 14th 1844  
> William Henry was born in Richland Jackson Co Iowa Oct 4th 1846  
> Albert Lorenzo was born in Richland Jackson Co Iowa Jan 11th 1850  
> Byram Theodore was born in Dodge Dubuque co Iowa May 19 1853  
> Francis Reed was born in Dodge Dubuque Co Iowa June 26th 1855  
> Clara Emma was born in Dodge Dubuque Co Iowa June 15th 1858

**Marriages**

> James S Dupray and Caroline M Chamberlain were married in Baveria[sic] in Portage Co Ohio May 24h 1842

**Deaths (excerpt)**

> Caroline M Dupray wife of James died Feb 22, 1865  
> James S Dupray died Dec 6th 1876
