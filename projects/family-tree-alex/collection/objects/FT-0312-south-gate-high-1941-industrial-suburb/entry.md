## What the city history says

From the City of South Gate “History of South Gate” page:

- South Gate High cornerstone laid **25 Jan 1930**; Floyd Honn first principal; opened Sept **1930** with 500 ninth/tenth graders and 27 faculty. Became a **six-year** school (grades 7–12) in **1932**. Remained so until **1941**, when **South Gate Junior High** opened on Firestone at Otis — the year of Richard’s class.
- Civic center: WPA library murals; new City Hall first used by council **19 Feb 1942** (war underway).
- Industry that defined the blue-collar suburb: **Firestone** tire plant (first tire **15 Jun 1928** on a former bean field); **General Motors** assembly from **1936** (Pontiac / Oldsmobile / Buick; workforce climbing toward thousands); Weiser hardware foundry; Maas Chemical; Star Roofing / U.S. Gypsum.
- Early volunteer fire department organized **1923** (Chief Edward McCormick) — separate from Richard’s later paid fire career in other cities.

Alumni yearbook project (sghsalumni.com): *Rambleback* yearbooks exist for **1941** (access noted; senior pages not posted open online this dig).
