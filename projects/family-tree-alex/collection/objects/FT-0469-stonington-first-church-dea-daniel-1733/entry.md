## Catalog note

**The sword-heir’s son becomes deacon of the same Stonington church.** Capt. John’s son **Daniel** (b. 1680 on the Mystic farm) was chosen deacon **5 Jul 1733** with Thomas Miner, “this Church being destitute of deacons since the formation of Mr. Eells’s Church.” That is the office Baldwin calls him **Dea. Daniel**. The 1874 book that called George the Miles Standish of the settlement is here naming the grandson in the deacons’ roll. Stone (FAG 71196292): “In Memory of Deacon Daniel Denison died October 13th 1747 Aged 67 years.”

## Excerpt

> No church history would be measurably complete without at least a brief notice of its deacons. … the men who have held that position in this Church were prominent among their fellow-men, and magnified the office by well ordered lives.

> July 5, 1733, Daniel Denison and Thomas Miner were chosen deacons ; this Church being destitute of deacons since the formation of Mr. Eells’s Church.

Later same list (not this Daniel): Joseph Denison, Esq. 1748; Ebenezer Denison junior deacon 1824 — later Denisons on the same board; do not merge them with Dea. Daniel Sr.

## Supports

- Supports: Daniel Denison chosen deacon of Stonington First Church **5 Jul 1733**, church previously without deacons after the Eells split
- Does not support: Date he first joined; sermons; that later Joseph/Ebenezer Denison deacons are the same man; Jane Cogswell / Abigail Fish later wives as story (not chased)
