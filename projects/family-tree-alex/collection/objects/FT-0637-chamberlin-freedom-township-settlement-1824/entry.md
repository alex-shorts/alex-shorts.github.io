## Catalog note

**Secondary / local-historian.** Compiled Portage County Chamberlain genealogy and Freedom Township historical materials place **Ira Chamberlin** and **Salome (Barnes) Chamberlin** among pioneer families who **settled Freedom Township in 1824** — the township where daughter **Mary Ann (Chamberlin) Dupray** and sister **Caroline M. Chamberlin** were born and raised before Ohio marriages to the Dupray line. Salome d. **12 Sep 1862** Freedom; buried Freedom West Cemetery per compiled vitals (**FT-0534** tier).

## Full text

**Chamberlain family compilation (BYU openEQUELLA title):**

> *The Chamberlain family of Portage County, Ohio : the decendants of Ira and Salome Chamberlain who settled Freedom Township in 1824*

**Freedom Township, Portage County (historical society / township site):**

> Freedom Township… has a rich history dating back to **1818** when Charles Paine… became its first permanent settler. Originally known as "North Rootstown"… the township was officially organized in **1825**… According to local tradition, the name "Freedom" was chosen by Charles Paine's wife to reflect the ideals of liberty and opportunity found on the Ohio frontier. Early settlers faced rugged conditions, with much of the area consisting of dense forests and swamps… Through determination and hard work, these pioneers transformed the land into productive farms…

**Salome Barnes Chamberlin (compiled vitals corroborating Freedom residence):**

> Born **23 Dec 1782** Canaan, Litchfield, Connecticut… d. **12 Sep 1862** Freedom, Portage, Ohio… buried Freedom West Cemetery… Daughter **Mary Ann Chamberlin** (1823–1884).
