## Nemaha County, Nebraska — surname neighbors (do not merge)

Andreas, *History of the State of Nebraska* (Nemaha Co.) describes a thrifty **German** settlement in Benton precinct from **1856**, naming **W. Tuxhorn** and **John Tuxhorn** among first settlers; first marriage **John Tuxhorn × Cora Williams (1857)**; Evangelical Lutheran preaching from 1858; church 1866.

Local trail history adds frontier color from the same settlement:

> …barley coffee and sorghum molasses tasted good… The first threshing machine… as late as 1864. John Tuxcorn, W. Williams, William Tuxcorn… stacked their wheat, oats, rye and barley all in the same yard, and it took less than one day to thresh the whole setting.

**Confidence:** High that Nemaha Tuxhorns were German prairie pioneers. **Low / unproven** that they share Frederick’s Dornberg→McPherson immigrant line — treat as **FAN / surname parallel**, not soft-ingest parents.
