## Full text

### Bigelow Society (Ebenezer 3)

Debra Bigelow, daughter of Ebenezer 3 ( Joshua 2, John 1) and Hannah (Browne) Bigelow, was born between 1724 and 1730. She married (about 1740's) Joel Pringle (Prindle?), born in New Haven, CT 1725 and moved to Danbury at a young age. He did eventually have land in Skenesborough (Whitehall), NY. He and his children came to the Kingston area as United empire Loyalists. Debra died age 65 years, 8 mo., 8 da., and is buried in the Loyalist cemetery at Adolphustown, Ontario.
