## Catalog note

**History lesson:** American Revolution → United Empire Loyalists → Upper Canada land grants & Bay of Quinte settlement.  
**Family hinge:** Museum story for the Denison Christian Cross quilt (FT-0055) names Elizabeth’s father **William Thompson** as a UEL who received a **Fredericksburg Township** grant **1790**; Elizabeth married **Nathan Denison** (Mystic CT → Richmond Twp); daughter **Mary Anne** finishes the quilt **1890** — Probable identity with graph Mary Ann Shorts via Napanee FAN.

## Museum Object Story (verbatim beats)

> Her father, William Thompson was a United Empire Loyalist who received a land grant in Fredericksburg Township in 1790. Elizabeth’s Mother was Magdalene Keller. … Elizabeth Married Nathan Denison, who was born March 2, 1781 in Mystic, Connecticut. … Nathan passed away November 16, 1849 in Richmond Township, meanwhile Elizabeth lived until November 19, 1873, in Napanee. … There is an inscription in one of the open crosses for the year 1890, after Elizabeth had died, likely indicating that the quilt had been left unfinished and was taken up by Mary Anne Denison, Elizabeth’s daughter.
