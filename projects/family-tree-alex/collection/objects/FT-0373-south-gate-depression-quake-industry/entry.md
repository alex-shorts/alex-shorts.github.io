## LA County Library / city beats

From South Gate Local History FAQ + city History page:

- Planned South Gate Gardens sales from **1917**; city incorporated **1923**.
- **Great Depression:** “severe financial difficulties brought on in part by unwise spending decisions.”
- **1933** earthquake (**6.3**): damaged schools, businesses, and homes; **five** killed in South Gate.
- Industry as founding strategy: Maas Chemical **1922**, Bell Foundry **1923**, **Firestone** on a bean field, Royal Dairy; 1930s arrivals include Star Roofing / U.S. Gypsum, **General Motors**, American Concrete & Steel Pipe.
- By **1940**, **35+** factories; WWII “hive of activity” with war workers around the clock; many stayed after 1945.
- Hollydale (annexed): Depression left scattered houses; WPA gardens in empty lots; WWII factories + anti-aircraft gun near knitting mill turned aircraft parts.
