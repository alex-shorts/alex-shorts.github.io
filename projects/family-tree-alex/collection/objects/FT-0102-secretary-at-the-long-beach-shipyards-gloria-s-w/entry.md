## What “Long Beach shipyards” meant

During WWII the Los Angeles–Long Beach harbor complex (Terminal Island / San Pedro Bay) became one of the nation’s densest shipbuilding zones:

- **California Shipbuilding Corporation (Calship)** on Terminal Island built hundreds of **Liberty** and Victory cargo ships; peak workforce cited around **40,000** men and women, most with no prior shipyard experience.
- Nearby yards (Todd / Bethlehem / Navy dry docks) repaired and built warships and merchant hulls for the Pacific.

Clerical staff — secretaries, payroll, scheduling — kept mass production moving. Gloria’s title is **secretary**, not welder; the open histories still place her in that wartime/postwar industrial surge.
