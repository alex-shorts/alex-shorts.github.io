## Catalog note

Civic close of the life: he donated the **first burying-ground** in southern Heath (**1791**). He died at sea (**14 Oct 1799**); grandchildren raised a family monument **4 Jul 1859** in South Cemetery — a **cenotaph**. Brother Benjamin sold land for the **second** burial ground the same year the first was enclosed (**1798**).

## Full text

### Everts 1879 — Heath cemeteries

> The first burying-ground was opened in the southern part of the town, on land donated by Col. Hugh Maxwell, in 1791, and, in 1798, Hugh Maxwell, Jr., Roger Leavitt, and James White were appointed a committee to inclose this ground. The same year an acre of ground was purchased of Benjamin Maxwell, near Joseph Wilder's place, north of the centre of the town, for the second burial-place.

Source page: http://www.franklincountyhistory.com/heath/everts/08.html

### Find a Grave 98874139 — inscription (cenotaph)

Individual stone (partial):

> In memory of Esq Hugh Maxwell he died Oct 1799 in the 67th year of his age

MAXWELL family monument, south side (as transcribed on the memorial):

> A soldier and an officer in the French war from 1755 to 1764 Escaped the massacre at Ft Wm Henry 1756 a member of the first Provencial Conress 1744 [sic — 1774] A brave and faithful officer in the war of the Revolution from April 1775 to April 1784 Was at the seige of Boston and the Retreat from Long Island and New York fought at Bunkerhill, Trenton, Princeton, Saratoga and Monmouth Suffered in the camps at Morristown and Valley Forge Watched for three years on the lines near New York under General Heath who said of him "Often I have slept without fear of being surprised, because I knew you were at the outpost." in civil life he obtained the carter for this town was the first justice commissioned in this section, the first deacon in the First Church in this town A Christian Patriot and Christian Soldier Honored his God, served his country, Loved his family To duty was ever true To his posterity His memory is a rich inheritance may they emulate his virtue

Gravesite note on FAG:

> "This Monument was erected by his Grandchildren July 4, 1859"

FAG also notes spouse Bridget Monro Maxwell (1735–1816) and parents Hugh (1701–1759) — graph elder Hugh already held.
