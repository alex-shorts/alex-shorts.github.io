## Catalog note

Widow of **Dr. Erastus J. Buck**; d. Seattle **25 Sep 1943** age **97**; cremated, remains to **Greenwood Cemetery**, Platteville. Surviving **six daughters**; names deceased husband, son **Guerdon C. Buck** (1932), no grandchildren named.

## Full text

**Platteville Journal and Grant County News, 30 Sep 1943:**

> Mrs. Sarah Trask Buck, a former resident, passed away Saturday morning at Seattle, Washington. A highly respected citizen, she lived a useful life to the ripe old age of 97 years, the past three of those years being bedridden as the result of a broken hip.
>
> Mrs. Buck was the widow of Dr. Erastus J. Buck, a Civil War surgeon. She was born in South Jefferson, Maine, **May 12, 1846**. For a time, she taught English in the Old Normal here. From Platteville, Mrs. Buck had a long residence in Milwaukee, and for the past 8 years has resided in Seattle.
>
> Surviving are six daughters: **Gertrude Buck** and **Mrs. Florence Saxton** of Seattle, Wash., **Mrs. Fred Hallett**, Chamberlain, S.D.; **Berniece Buck**, Hammond, Ind.; **Mrs. Clara Nehls**, Buffalo, N.Y. and **Mrs. Winifred Muckler**, Missoula, Mont. Her husband passed away a number of years ago and her only son, **Guerdon C. Buck**, Platteville, in **1932**.
>
> Funeral services were held Monday afternoon at Seattle. The body will be cremated and brought to Platteville for burial in Greenwood Cemetery the latter part of the week.
