## Full text

**Joel “Pringle” Prindle Sr.** (28 Jan 1696 – 21 Feb 1726)

- **Birth:** 28 Jan 1696, New Haven, New Haven County, Connecticut, USA  
- **Death:** 21 Feb 1726 (aged 30), New Haven, New Haven County, Connecticut, USA  
- **Burial:** West Haven Green Cemetery, West Haven, New Haven County, Connecticut, USA  
- **Memorial ID:** 59610755  

Joel Prindle (or Pringle) was born to Joseph Prindle (1663–1737) and Mary Brown (1664–1704) in New Haven, Connecticut.

**Baptized:** 15 Mar 1695, First Congregational Church Center Church on the Green Churchyard, New Haven.

**Husband of:** Jemima Beecher Benham (m. 15 Oct 1718), New Haven.

**Interment:** 23 Feb 1726.

**Children:**

1. William (1719–1747)  
2. Sarah (1721–1750), m. Andrew Ives  
3. Lois (1724–1756), m. Henry Bristol  
4. Joel II (1725–1800), m. Deborah Bigelow  

### Inscription (stone)

HERE LYETH THE BODY OF JOEL PRINDLE WHO DIED FEB 21 1726 AGED 34 YEARS

(Stone age 34 vs memorial age 30 — record both; WikiTree holds 23 Feb death.)
