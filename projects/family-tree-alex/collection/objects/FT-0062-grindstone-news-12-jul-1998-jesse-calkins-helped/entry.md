## Catalog note

Island column that names a **named helper** on Alexander Shorts’s church build: Jesse Calkins (son of Rev. John Calkins), read from 1989–90 family diaries by Aminta Marks with Ramona and Lyle Nunn. Also situates Audrey Lashomb’s *Going Home* as a church-centered book then at press.

## Full text (relevant excerpt)

**Grindstone Island News — July 12, 1998**  
Aminta Marks

> John and I had one of those pleasant times together with Ramona and Lyle Nunn over on Wellesley Island last week. While John and Lyle talked, Ramona and I read the 1989 and 1990 diaries of the Rev. John Calkins, great, great grandfather to Lyle. I was interested in them because his son, **Jesse helped the Rev. Shorts build Grindstone Island’s church**, and the church, of course, figures importantly in Audrey’s book. Decisions on big things like color or no color for the photographs of Audrey’s *Going Home* have been made this week …(color on the cover only) …so now we just need to add a few last details and by Tuesday Audrey can simply sit and wait for Mary Curtis Emerick, the printer, to get it back to us all finished and ready for the bookstores.

(Column continues with church supper/auction, sanctuary-refurbish discussion, children’s choir, Dodge Hall bluebird meeting, etc.)
