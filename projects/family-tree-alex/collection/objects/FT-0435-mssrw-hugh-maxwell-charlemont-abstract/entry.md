## Catalog note

Corroborates Heitman ladder with muster/pay detail: Valley Forge company return **24 Jan 1778** (still listed Captain while paid as Major), West Point rank board **1779**, Col. M. ## Full text

### MAXWELL, HUGH, Charlemont (vol. 10)

> MAXWELL, HUGH, Charlemont. Major, in a regiment of Minute-men commanded by Lieut. Col. Samuel Williams; service from April 21, 1775 [OCR prints 1776; alarm year], 19 days; also, communication dated Cambridge, May 25, 1775, signed by said Maxwell and others, captains in Col. William Prescott's regt., stating their approval of William Prescott, John Robinson, and Henry Woods as Colonel, Lieutenant Colonel, and Major, respectively, of said regiment, and asking that they be commissioned; also, Captain, Col. William Prescott's regt.; return of officers, dated May 25, 1775; ordered in Provincial Congress May 26, 1775, that said officers be commissioned; also, Captain, Col. Prescott's regt.; muster roll dated Aug. 1, 1775; engaged May 10, 1775; service, 2 mos. 23 days; also, company return [probably Oct., 1775]; also, Major, Col. Bailey's regt.; Continental Army pay accounts for service from Jan. 1, 1777, to Dec. 31, 1779; reported as serving 6 mos. as Captain, 30 mos. as Major; also, Captain, 1st co., same regt.; company return dated Camp near Valley Forge, Jan. 24, 1778; also, order on the Board of War… dated Camp White Plains, Aug. 19, 1778…; also… Col. Bailey's (2d) regt.; list of officers, dated Boston, May 19, 1779; also, Major; list of settlements of rank of Continental officers, dated West Point… confirmed by Congress Sept. 6, 1779; commissioned July 7, 1777; also… said Maxwell reported Major on the new establishment; also, Major, 2d Mass. regt. commanded by Lieut. Colonel Ezra Badlam… Dec. 7, 1779; also, Major, Col. Bailey's regt.; Continental Army pay accounts… Jan. 1, 1780, to Dec. 31, 1780; also, letter from M. Jackson, Colonel, 8th Mass. regt., to the Governor and Council, dated Camp Yerplanck's Point, Oct. 15, 1782, requesting that warrants be issued… said Maxwell, senior Major, recommended for Lieutenant Colonel; advised in Council Oct. 29, 1782, that warrants be granted; also, Lieutenant Colonel, 3d Mass. regt. commanded by Col. Michael Jackson; list of officers [year not given]; reported absent.

## Notes

- Alarm-year OCR “1776” in Minute-man line is **1775** (Lexington week).
- Valley Forge **24 Jan 1778** return still styles him Captain of 1st co. while pay accounts already count Major months — bureaucracy lag, not a demotion.
- Jackson recommendation **15 Oct 1782** / Council **29 Oct 1782** aligns with Heitman Lt. Col. **1 Aug 1782** (rank date vs warrant paperwork).
