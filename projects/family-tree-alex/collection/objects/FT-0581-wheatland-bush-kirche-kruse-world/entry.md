## St. John's — "Bush Kirche" (excerpt)

From [St. John's Wheatland church history](https://iagenweb.org/allamakee/church/StJohns_WheatlandHx.htm):

> The founders of St. John's came from **Hanover and Westphalia, Germany**. When the sea voyage of these good people had ended, they set foot on land where they were strangers, and faced a trek across rugged hills and valleys, following in the trail of relatives and friends who had gone before them. The pioneer forefathers of St. John's settled in the **northeast corner of Iowa and the southeast portion of Minnesota**, near the banks of the **Mississippi River**. The church was built in 1880 on a level site in the **"bush"** in Union City Township… and was known as the **"Bush Kirche."** After the "bush" was gone much wheat was raised, and this area thereafter has been called **Wheatland**.

Further color from the same history: first pastor dedicated the church **15 Aug 1880**; confirmation class **Palm Sunday 1881**; German language services until **1935** when English alternated every other Sunday; **Kinderfest** (Children's Day) in the woods from **1899**.
