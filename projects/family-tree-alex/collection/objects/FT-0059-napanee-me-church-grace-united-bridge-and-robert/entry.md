## Catalog note

Places Alexander’s **1881 Napanee (Lennox)** ME census charge at a concrete building: the **Methodist Episcopal Church** shown on the **1874** Napanee map at the corner of **Robert and Bridge streets**, now **Grace United Church**.

## Full text

### South Fredericksburgh / L&A County heritage sketch (1967)

…A map drawn in 1874, recently reproduced by the county historical society, shows Napanee has retained almost the same dimensions during the past 90 years. Churches in 1874 included the church of England, (St. Mary Magdalene’s Anglican Church); the Presbyterian Church (the Masonic Temple at the corner of Bridge street and Belleville road); the **Methodist episcopal Church (now Grace United church at the corner of Robert and Bridge streets)**; Wesleyan Methodist at John and Bridge streets (now Trinity United Church) and St. Patrick’s Roman Catholic Church on West street at Thomas street.

### Greater Napanee Historic Walking Tour — Stop 47. Grace United Church

The church, built in a Gothic Revival style, features a tall graceful spire visible from many locations in Town. It features lancet windows, hood molds over windows, buttresses and a steeply pitched roof.

Grace United address cluster (open church site): corner of Bridge and Robert Streets, Napanee.
