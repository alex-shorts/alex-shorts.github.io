## Full text

> The next year, Hugh Maxwell was sent as Charlemont’s representation to the Provincial Congress in 1774. Early in 1775, a committee was formed in Charlemont to track the activities of the Continental Congress.

> According to White’s book, “a company of minute men was early formed,” under Capt. Oliver Avery with then Lt. Maxwell. With how large that company already was, a second group of men from Charlemont, totaling 56 individuals, formed under Col. William Prescott, with Maxwell rising to the rank of captain. Even more men from Charlemont volunteered and joined Prescott’s company.

> Like Hawley, the name of the town of Heath has a Revolutionary War connection. The town is named after William Heath, major general of Massachusetts and brigadier general in the national army during the American Revolution. This name came as a suggestion from then-Charlemont and future Heath resident Hugh Maxwell, who served in the Continental Army with Heath and was a close friend.

> Percy Whiting Brown… surmises that Aaron Barr, a young man from then-Myrifield, was the first man killed during the Battle of Bunker Hill. Barr was a soldier underneath Capt. Hugh Maxwell of Charlemont.
