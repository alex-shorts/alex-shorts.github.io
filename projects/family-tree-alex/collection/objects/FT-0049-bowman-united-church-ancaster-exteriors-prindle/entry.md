## Full text

### Historical Hamilton site note (gallery page)

Bowman church in Ancaster is on Garner Road. The building has orange bricks. There is a cemetery found behind, on both sides of it. There are some interesting old headstones in the graveyard. Date Built: 1881.

### Prindle relic / burial (open narrative already at FT-0032)

From fadedgenes (Mrs. A. McCulloch tradition), not reminted as a second object for that URL:

> The Rev. Andrew Prindle (1780-1855) was minister at Bowman Church Ancaster in 1809, and, after his superannuation, retired to Ancaster. He became so fat in middle life that a special buggy, with extra strong springs, had to be built for him, and a very wide chair was made for him. This unique chair it still preserved in Bowman Church as a prized relic of this popular preacher. … Andrew Prindle and his wife lie buried on the east side of Bowman Church. In 1935, a cairn was built to hold his fallen tombstone, and a memorial service was conducted by the Rev. T. Albert Moore…
