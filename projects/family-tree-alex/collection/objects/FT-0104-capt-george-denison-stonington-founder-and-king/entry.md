## What open sources say

Baldwin & Clift (**1881**) open with Capt. George × Ann Borodell and the Stonington Denison stem. WikiTree **Denison-13** (sourced synopsis) places him among the **1654** founders of Stonington (with Chesebrough, Palmer, Minor, Stanton); **Great Swamp Fight** (19 Dec 1675) with New London County forces; later forays as Provost Marshal; capture of Narragansett sachem **Canonchet** (Apr 1676) brought to Stonington. Died **23 Oct 1694** Hartford while serving as deputy; buried Ancient Burying Ground; Elm Grove memorial at Mystic.

NPS NRHP text for **Denison Homestead / Pequotsepos Manor** (later house on the Captain’s site): Capt. George granted ~200 acres after New London service; fought in King Philip’s War and the Canonchet episode; present museum house built **1717** after an earlier house burned.
