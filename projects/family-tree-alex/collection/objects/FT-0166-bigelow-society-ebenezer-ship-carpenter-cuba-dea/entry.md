## Full text

### Bigelow Society Ebenezer³

> He was a carpenter by trade. His father sold him a piece of land in Weston 07 October 1717. He lived in Weston a short time, then moved to Worcester, then later to Newton, and by 1740 was living in Waltham. **Family tradition says he died in Cuba** (see below).

> 16A1+ Ebenezer, b 23 Jan 1721 Weston; **d between 1756 and 1758 on the expedition to Canada** …

### Joshua² child note (ship carpenter)

> Ebenezer was a **ship's carpenter**, and is said to have died in Cuba.

### Malburg query (on Ebenezer³ page)

> most of the family histories say that Ebenezer is said to have died in Cuba. I found two sources that show he was in the Colonial Military and in fact **died in the Battle of Ticonderoga** in the French and Indian War.
