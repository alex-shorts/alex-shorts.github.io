The Temple · image stack

God’s dwelling — from garden kingdom to glory-filled house to a jewel-temple of living stones.

Garden Temple

Genesis 2:8–15

  1. What work does God give Adam in the garden?
  2. How does their service relate to God’s kingdom on earth?
Priest-kings in the garden — establishing God’s kingdom on earth

Story: Before the fall, God places Adam and Eve in Eden’s garden-sanctuary to serve and keep it — a majestic commission to extend God’s reign from the garden outward. Cast: Adam and Eve as priest-kings; the garden-temple; tree and river. Turn: they stand shrouded in glory-light, serving — kingdom work in the first sanctuary.

House for the Name

1 Kings 8:10–13

  1. What fills the house so the priests cannot stand to minister?
  2. What does Solomon say God has done for his dwelling?
From tent to house — weighty glory fills the dwelling

Story: Solomon dedicates the temple; priests cannot stand to minister because the cloud fills the house of the LORD. Cast: Solomon; priests; glory-cloud. Turn: the permanent house is filled — same glory, larger sanctuary.

This Temple

John 2:13–22

  1. How is Jesus’ answer both a rebuke and a prophecy?
  2. What temple is he speaking about — and what is coming?
A rebuke and a prophecy — destroy this temple; raise it in three days

Story: Jesus answers with rebuke and prophecy: “Destroy this temple, and in three days I will raise it up” — speaking of his body; hearers are confused, thinking only of the stone house, while the cross and rising are already in view. Cast: Jesus; confused listeners; stone temple; foreshadow of death and rising. Turn: rebuke lands; prophecy points beyond their grasp to what is coming.

Living Stones

Ephesians 2:19–22; cf. Revelation 21:11, 18–21

  1. Who is the cornerstone of God’s household?
  2. How does Christ’s glory shine through his living stones?
Pure living stones — Christ’s glory shines through in many colors

Story: God builds his church as a holy temple; like Revelation’s pure jewel-stones, living stones are translucent to the light of Christ — different colors reflecting aspects of his glory shining through his people. Cast: living stones / jewel-people; Christ the cornerstone / light-source. Turn: the house he builds transmits his light — glory refracted in many colors.

New City Catechism · Q48

Q: What is the church?

A: God chooses and preserves for himself a community elected for eternal life and united by faith, who love, follow, learn from, and worship God together as his people. God sends us out as his witnesses and to make disciples.

How it fits this image: Living temple / household of God — the people where God dwells by his Spirit (Eph 2; 1 Cor 3). Alternate Q20: the Redeemer is Jesus — the true temple (John 2).

Teacher notes · further typology

Thematic banner

Copywork: 1 Corinthians 3:16 ESV. Majestic arc: Eden priest-kings in kingdom light → Solomon’s weighty glory → Jesus’ rebuke-prophecy → Revelation-pure living stones transmitting Christ’s multicolored glory. Stack G1 G3 G6 G8.

Richer typology (optional discussion)

  • Eden pre-fall: Adam and Eve as priest-kings establishing God’s kingdom on earth in the garden-sanctuary (Gen 2; Beale) — shrouded in glory-light as they serve.
  • Solomon’s house filled with weighty glory — priests cannot stand (1 Kgs 8).
  • Jesus’ “Destroy this temple…” is rebuke and prophecy: hearers confuse the stone house; he speaks of his body and what is coming (John 2:19–22).
  • Church as living temple (Eph 2; 1 Cor 3) seen through Revelation’s pure jewel-stones — translucent to Christ’s light; different colors = aspects of his glory (Rev 21:11, 18–21).
  • River from the temple (Ezek 47) — presence meant to overflow; stack plate G8.
