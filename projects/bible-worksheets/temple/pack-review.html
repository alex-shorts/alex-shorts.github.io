2. Stories — pick 4

C1Recommended

Garden Temple — Genesis 2:8–15; 3:24

Beat: God plants Eden as his first sanctuary; Adam is to serve and guard it; after the fall, cherubim bar the way.

Cast: Adam and Eve · the LORD God · cherubim at the east · Turn: Presence fills a garden — then holy exclusion.

Panel: Eden gate with tree/river medallion and cherubim guarding; garden-as-temple, not a stone building.

Connection: The story of the temple begins in a garden.

Coloring: Soft garden greens + gold presence-light; cherubim resolute, not grotesque; no gore.

C2Recommended

Glory Fills the Tent — Exodus 40:34–38

Beat: Moses finishes the tabernacle; the cloud covers it and the glory of the LORD fills the tent so Moses cannot enter.

Cast: Moses · Israel camped around · cloud of glory · Turn: God moves in — the tent becomes holy dwelling.

Panel: Tabernacle silhouette under a bright glory-cloud; people small at the edge.

Connection: God chooses to dwell with his people on the way.

Coloring: Gold cloud over ochre tent; keep forms simple and readable.

C3

House for the Name — 1 Kings 8:10–13

Beat: Solomon dedicates the temple; priests cannot stand to minister because the cloud fills the house of the LORD.

Cast: Solomon · priests · glory-cloud · Turn: The permanent house is filled — same glory, larger sanctuary.

Panel: Temple facade with pillars; glory-cloud pouring into the doorway.

Connection: From tent to house — God still comes near.

Coloring: Geometric temple + soft cloud; avoid ornate interior clutter.

C4

River from the Threshold — Ezekiel 47:1–12

Beat: Ezekiel sees water flowing from under the temple threshold; it deepens to a river that heals and brings life to trees.

Cast: Ezekiel · temple doorway · deepening river · trees · Turn: Life pours out from God’s house to the land.

Panel: Temple threshold with golden light; river widening toward the viewer; trees on banks.

Connection: God’s presence is meant to overflow outward.

Coloring: Teal river, soft trees; doorway as light source — no scary vision faces.

C5Recommended

This Temple — John 2:13–22

Beat: Jesus clears the temple courts; challenged, he says “Destroy this temple, and in three days I will raise it up” — speaking of his body.

Cast: Jesus · money-changers · disciples · Turn: The true meeting-place with God is Jesus himself.

Panel: Jesus standing before a temple silhouette, open hands; courts suggested simply behind.

Connection: The stone house points to a living temple — Christ.

Coloring: Reverent figure + temple outline; no violence or chaos spectacle.

C6

Living Stones — Ephesians 2:19–22

Beat: Believers are no longer strangers but members of God’s household, built on the apostles and prophets, Christ the cornerstone — a holy temple in the Lord.

Cast: Household of faith · cornerstone Christ · Spirit dwelling · Turn: People become the sanctuary.

Panel: Simplified figures forming a house outline with gold light at the center (Christ).

Connection: The temple expands into a people filled with God’s Spirit.

Coloring: Clear human silhouettes as architecture; warm center light; calm, not crowded.

C7Recommended

No Temple There — Revelation 21:1–3, 22

Beat: John sees new heaven and earth and the holy city; the dwelling of God is with man; he sees no temple in the city — its temple is the Lord God and the Lamb.

Cast: John · New Jerusalem · God and the Lamb · Turn: Presence fills everything — no separate building needed.

Panel: Glowing cube-city with river and tree of life; radiant presence-light; no free-standing temple.

Connection: Eden’s hope ends in a garden-city where God dwells fully.

Coloring: Jewel ochres + deep blue; soft radiance; meditative, not scary apocalypse.

C8

Altar Flags on the Land — Genesis 12:7–8; 28:10–22

Beat: Abraham and Jacob mark places with altars and tent-shrines where God appears — planting presence-claims across the land on the way to a permanent house.

Cast: Patriarchs · altars · Bethel vision · Turn: Small sanctuaries anticipate the greater dwelling.

Panel: Simple stone altar on a hill with soft presence-light; tent nearby.

Connection: Before the temple, God meets his people at altars.

Coloring: Sparse landscape, one altar, soft light; keep quiet and open.

4. Catechism

Q48Recommended

Q. What is the church?

A. God chooses and preserves for himself a community elected for eternal life and united by faith, who love, follow, learn from, and worship God together as his people. God sends us out as his witnesses and to make disciples.

Fit: Living temple / household of God — the people where God dwells by his Spirit (Eph 2; 1 Cor 3).

Q20

Q. Who is the Redeemer?

A. The only Redeemer is the Lord Jesus Christ, the eternal Son of God, in whom God became man and bore the penalty for sin himself.

Fit: Jesus is the true temple (John 2) — God with us in a person, not only a building.

Q6

Q. How can we glorify God?

A. We glorify God by enjoying him, loving him, trusting him, and by obeying his will, commands, and law.

Fit: Dwelling with God is the point of the temple story — presence enjoyed, not merely visited.
