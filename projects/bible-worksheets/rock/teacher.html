The Rock · historic images

The LORD is our rock — he provides, shelters, and builds us together on Christ.

Water from the Rock

Exodus 17:1–7

  1. What did Israel need that only God could give from a rock?
  2. How does Paul later name this Rock (1 Cor 10:4)?
God gave water from the rock; that Rock points to Christ

Story: At Rephidim Israel has no water; the LORD tells Moses to strike the rock at Horeb; water pours out. Cast: Moses with staff; wilderness rock; Israelites with jars. Turn: staff strikes — water where there was none.

The LORD My Rock

Psalm 18:1–3

  1. What does a rock give that sand or soft ground cannot?
  2. Where do you run when trouble comes — and where does David run?
The LORD himself is the safe rock and fortress

Story: David sings love to the LORD who rescued him — rock, fortress, deliverer, refuge. Cast: David beside a cliff-fortress rock; storm far off. Turn: he takes refuge in the LORD, not himself.

Jesus the Cornerstone

Matthew 21:42

  1. What did the builders do with the stone in Psalm 118?
  2. What does Jesus say about that stone when he quotes it?
Jesus claims to be the rejected stone made cornerstone

Story: In the temple courts Jesus quotes Psalm 118 — the rejected stone has become the cornerstone — applying it to himself. Cast: Jesus, cornerstone, a few leaders, temple-wall cue. Turn: he claims the cornerstone role.

Living Stones

1 Peter 2:4–5

  1. Who is the living stone we come to?
  2. What are we being built into together?
In Christ we are living stones built into God’s house

Story: Come to Christ the living stone; you yourselves like living stones are built into a spiritual house. Cast: living cornerstone; people fitted into a rising wall. Turn: scattered people gathered into one house.

New City Catechism · Q48

Q: What is the church?

A: God chooses and preserves for himself a community elected for eternal life and united by faith, who love, follow, learn from, and worship God together. God sends out this community to proclaim the gospel and prefigure Christ’s kingdom by the quality of their life together and their love for one another.

How it fits this image: Living stones built into a spiritual house — the church gathered on Christ the cornerstone.

Teacher notes · further typology

Thematic banner

Psalm 62:2 — rock / fortress / unshaken; week arc: provision → refuge → Jesus the stone → we built into the temple.

Richer typology (optional discussion)

  • Wilderness rock (Exod 17; Num 20) → “and the Rock was Christ” (1 Cor 10:4).
  • LORD as rock / fortress (Pss 18; 62; Deut 32:4) → personal trust language for children.
  • Ps 118:22 rejected cornerstone → Jesus claims it of himself (Matt 21:42; cf. Acts 4:11).
  • Christ the living stone + believers as living stones built into a spiritual house/temple (1 Pet 2:4–5; Eph 2:19–22).

Q29: How can we be saved? — Only by faith in Jesus Christ and in his substitutionary atoning death on the cross; so even though we are guilty of having disobeyed God and are still inclined to all evil, nevertheless, God, without any merit of our own but only by pure grace, imputes to us the perfect righteousness of Christ when we repent and believe in him.
