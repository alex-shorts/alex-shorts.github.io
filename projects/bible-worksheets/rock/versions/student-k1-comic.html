Biblical Imagery

The Rock

Isaiah 26:4 · ESV

Trust in the LORD forever, for the LORD GOD is an everlasting rock.

1

Water from the Rock

Exodus 17:1–7
  1. What did Israel need that only God could give from a rock?
  2. How does Paul later name this Rock (1 Cor 10:4)?
2

The LORD Is the Rock

Deuteronomy 32:4; 2 Samuel 22:2
  1. What does it mean that God's work is perfect as the Rock (Deut 32:4)?
  2. Where does David run when enemies press him (2 Sam 22)?
3

House on the Rock

Matthew 7:24–27
  1. What is the difference between hearing Jesus and doing what he says?
  2. Why does the house on the rock stand when the storm comes?
4

Living Water from the Rock

John 7:37–39; Ezekiel 47:1
  1. What did Jesus promise on the last day of the feast (John 7)?
  2. Where does Ezekiel's vision show water beginning to flow?

New City Catechism · Q2

Q: What is God?

A: God is the creator and sustainer of everyone and everything. He is eternal, infinite, and unchangeable in his power and perfection, goodness and glory, wisdom, justice, and truth. No one is as great as God.
