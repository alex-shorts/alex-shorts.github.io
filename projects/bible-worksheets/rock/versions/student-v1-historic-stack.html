Biblical Imagery

The Rock

Psalm 62:2 · ESV

He alone is my rock and my salvation, my fortress; I shall not be greatly shaken.

Historic images

The LORD is our rock — he provides, shelters, and builds us together on Christ.

1

Water from the Rock Exodus 17:1–7

  1. What did Israel need that only God could give from a rock?
  2. How does Paul later name this Rock (1 Cor 10:4)?
2

The LORD My Rock Psalm 18:1–3

  1. What does a rock give that sand or soft ground cannot?
  2. Where do you run when trouble comes — and where does David run?
3

Jesus the Cornerstone Matthew 21:42

  1. What did the builders do with the stone in Psalm 118?
  2. What does Jesus say about that stone when he quotes it?
4

Living Stones 1 Peter 2:4–5

  1. Who is the living stone we come to?
  2. What are we being built into together?

New City Catechism · Q48

Q: What is the church?

A: God chooses and keeps a people united by faith who love, follow, learn from, and worship him together — and sends them to share the gospel and show Christ’s love.
