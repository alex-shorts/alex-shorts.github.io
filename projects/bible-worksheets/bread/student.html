Biblical Imagery

The Bread

John 6:35 · ESV

I am the bread of life; whoever comes to me shall not hunger, and whoever believes in me shall never thirst.

Historic images

God feeds his people — from heaven’s manna to the Bread of Life who gives himself.

1

Bread from Heaven Exodus 16:4–15

  1. What does God say the manna will test — and what must Israel do each morning?
  2. How is this bread “from heaven,” yet still something they gather with their hands?
2

Bread of the Presence Leviticus 24:5–9

  1. Where does this bread sit, and how often is it renewed?
  2. Who may eat it — and what does that say about nearness to God?
3

Five Loaves for Thousands John 6:5–14

  1. What does Jesus do with the boy’s loaves before the people eat?
  2. What is left over — and what does that show about his providing?
4

Broken Bread Luke 22:19–20

  1. What does Jesus say the bread is, as he breaks it?
  2. How does this meal look back to Passover and forward to his death?

New City Catechism · Q46

Q: What is the Lord’s Supper?

A: Christ commanded all Christians to eat bread and to drink from the cup in thankful remembrance of him and his death. The Lord’s Supper celebrates God’s presence among us, feeds and nourishes our souls, and looks ahead to the feast in his kingdom.
