The Sword · image stack

God’s Word is living and sharp — his sword judges and saves.

Flaming Sword

Genesis 3:24

  1. What guards the way to the tree of life?
  2. Who places the cherubim and the flaming sword?
The sword bars the way back — holy judgment at the gate

Story: After the fall, God drives the man out and stations cherubim east of Eden with a flaming sword turning every way to guard the tree of life. Cast: Adam and Eve departing; cherubim at the gate; flaming sword. Turn: the way back is barred by holy fire and blade.

Sword Over Jerusalem

1 Chronicles 21:15–17

  1. What does David see in the angel’s hand?
  2. What word from the LORD stops the destruction?
The drawn sword hangs over the city — until mercy speaks

Story: After David’s census, the angel of the LORD stands between earth and heaven with a drawn sword stretched out over Jerusalem; David and the elders fall on their faces; the LORD says “Enough.” Cast: David in sackcloth; elders; angel with unsheathed sword. Turn: judgment raised — mercy stays the hand.

Sword in the Road

Numbers 22:31–35

  1. Who sees the angel’s sword first?
  2. What does the angel say he would have done to Balaam?
The drawn sword blocks a rebellious path

Story: The angel of the LORD blocks Balaam with a drawn sword; the donkey saw first; Balaam’s eyes are opened and he bows. Cast: Balaam; donkey; angel with drawn sword in the narrow path. Turn: opened eyes meet the unsheathed blade in the way.

Faithful and True

Revelation 19:11–16

  1. What is the rider’s robe dipped in?
  2. What names does the rider bear?
The sword of judgment belongs to Jesus — King of kings

Story: Heaven opens; a white horse; its rider called Faithful and True; his robe is dipped in blood; he wields a sharp sword dripping with blood; on his robe and thigh: King of kings and Lord of lords (Rev 19:11–16, esp. v.13). Cast: Rider on white horse; blood-dipped robe; dripping sword; heavenly armies behind. Turn: the warrior Word rides forth in judgment.

New City Catechism · Q5

Q: What else did God create?

A: God created all things by his powerful Word, and all his creation was very good; everything flourished under his loving rule.

How it fits this image: God’s Word creates and judges — living and active, sharper than a sword (Heb 4:12). Alternate Q20: the Redeemer is Jesus — the Rev 19 rider who wields the sharp sword.

Teacher notes · further typology

Thematic banner

The sword of the LORD — from Eden’s gate to the Word that discerns the heart to Christ the warrior-judge of Revelation 19.

Typology / continuity

  • Hebrews 4:12 — the Word as living two-edged sword; discerns the heart.
  • Gen 3:24 flaming sword — holy exclusion from the tree of life.
  • 1 Chr 21 / 2 Sam 24 — Angel of the LORD with drawn sword; mercy says “Enough.”
  • Num 22 — drawn sword in the path; the messenger blocks rebellion.
  • Rev 19:11–16 — Faithful and True; sharp sword from his mouth; King of kings.
