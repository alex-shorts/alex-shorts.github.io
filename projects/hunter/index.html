Why this cycle — a call to manhood and action

Summary: This year he learns what a man does with power over life and death — slowly, under mentors, on paper and on the range, then in the field. He earns hunter education, becomes safe and useful on rifle, handgun, and shotgun, shoots in real 4-H competition, takes a legal mentored deer hunt, and field-dresses what he takes. The family eats what he harvests. Win not required; waste, shortcuts, and theater are not allowed.

  • Food, not fantasy. Hunting is provision and responsibility — not combat cosplay, not unsupervised access, not carrying as a minor.
  • Skill before climax. Hunter ed and months of live-fire come before the trigger on a deer. January is the hunt; April is the scorecard; both matter.
  • Three platforms, one character. Rifle for the hunt and match precision; shotgun for birds and trap; handgun for discipline, patience, and the permission that law and coaches require.
  • Proof on paper. License, card, scorecard, tag, dress notes, and a family meal — so the story is true when he is older.
  • The prize is a gun of his own — earned, not given on day one. His choice from a shortlist you approve. Locked. Supervised only.

The call: show up weekly, obey range law, take the shot only when the mentor says yes, dress what you kill, and compete even when you are not the best shooter in the squad. That is the work of becoming a man who can be trusted with arms.

Optional frosting — US History I CLEP

His 8th-grade American history class already covers colonization through Reconstruction — the same span as CLEP History of the United States I → TJC HIST 1301 (3 college hours at score 50). Hillsdale films and Constitution 101 are the learning. This section is exam prep only. Run it after April so it does not steal time from the hunt, match, or dress. HS credit stands whether or not he passes.

What Modern States is

Modern States is a free nonprofit: short college-style lectures + readings + quizzes aligned to each CLEP exam. It is not a second history class — it teaches CLEP question style. Finish the course (75% quiz average + 75% on the course final) and Modern States pays the CLEP exam fee via a voucher. You still schedule the real exam at a testing center (~$0 exam + ~$20–30 site fee; voucher covers the exam). Account: age 13+. Course: History of the United States I.
