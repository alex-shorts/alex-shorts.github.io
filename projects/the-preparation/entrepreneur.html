11th-grade SparkSell + allocate

Why this combined cycle — earn, then steward

Entrepreneur without Investor is hustle without judgment. Investor without customers is theory. One year covers both — ops first, then capital allocation in writing.

  • Tyler SBDC + NGPF before inventory or trading.
  • Sell first: ten paying customers, weekly public posts.
  • Then allocate: tracked portfolio + thesis you can defend.
  • Off the list: fake customers, meme-stock gambling, no accounting.

The call: one more customer this week, one honest post, and every dollar of capital explained in writing.

Pick one offer. Narrow beats clever. Portfolio size can be small — the thesis is the proof.

Sell first, then allocate — ten customers plus a written investment thesis.
