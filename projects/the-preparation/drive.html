Why this cycle — control before speed

Summary: A man who cannot operate machines safely is a passenger in his own life. This year he earns seat time on small machines — throttle, clutch, weight transfer, panic recovery — then the legal right to drive on Texas roads, then a week of rally-school car control. Not kart leagues. Not race camps. Not mud-park chaos. The bar is the same as Hunter: proof on paper before the climax.

  • Beginner blocks only at 15. MSF DirtBike School teen, ASI ATV RiderCourse, K1-on-1 + DKC rental coaching — stacked days, not competition series.
  • License is a credential, not the whole story. Classroom at 14 · learner at 15 (6-month hold) · provisional at 16 · ITTD within 90 days of the driving test.
  • Team O'Neil Stage 1 is the climax — 5-day rally school in New Hampshire. Book after provisional is in hand. No license required for Stage 1; we still wait until 16.
  • Physics rides along. Motion, friction, vectors — HS physics intuition + optional GC. Algebra CLEP only if the math spine is already there.
  • Off the list: Underground Compound, Swan race training, DKC KA100/league, K1 Challenge GP, intermediate MX at Cycle Ranch.

The call: log every hour, finish what you start, never confuse a race fantasy for skill. Control the machine — then earn the road — then earn the rally week.

Summer before 9th (age 14): last window for kid camps — Iron Horse (ages 8–14) or Cycle Ranch Learn to Ride (7–13). Optional warm-up only; the real cycle starts with driver-ed classroom.
