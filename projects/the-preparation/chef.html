Why this cycle — hospitality under pressure

Italy-style imagery in the book; our version is East TX kitchen hours — stage, catering, or restaurant — plus food safety credentials.

  • Food safety first. Food Manager / ServSafe path (TJC CPL possible).
  • Stage or catering reps before the big service.
  • Climax: cook for 20+ · mentor eval · recipe book.
  • Off the list: one fancy dinner with no prep log.

The call: mise en place every time, taste as you go, and leave the kitchen cleaner than you found it.

Book the service date early. Guests and mentors need calendar notice.
