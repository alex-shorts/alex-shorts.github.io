Why this cycle — build something that stands

Preparation names house-building as an anchor skill. Our version is a real cabin raise — budget, CAD plan, photos at every stage, mentor sign-off. Not a birdhouse. Not a Pinterest shed.

  • OSHA-10 before power tools. TX DWC class — credential on file.
  • Onshape CAD — foundation plan before lumber hits the ground.
  • Frame → wire → dry-in with dad on our property.
  • Climax: weathertight + usable interior shell + before/after portfolio.
  • Off the list: skipping safety, buying finish granite before the roof dries in.

The call: measure twice, sweep the site every night, and sign your name to work you would trust your family to sleep inside.

Order materials before spring. Lumber lead times bite. Photo every stage for the portfolio.
