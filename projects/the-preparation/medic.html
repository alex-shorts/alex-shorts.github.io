Medic

Cycle 1 · 10th-grade Spark — CPR, Stop the Bleed, then a real First Responder or EMT path with documented hours on a ride-along or camp medic shift. Climax: cert card + hours log + after-action write-up.

10th-grade SparkCert + shift

Why this cycle — decisions when seconds count

Medic is Art of Manliness shortlist material. The adventure is real medical stress under supervision — not playing doctor on YouTube.

  • Credentials first. Red Cross CPR/First Aid · Stop the Bleed before clinical hours.
  • Age-gated clinical. First Responder or EMT course when eligible — TJC EMS path.
  • Climax: cert + hours log + one after-action from a real shift.
  • Off the list: unsupervised first aid on strangers, skipping the write-up.

The call: show up calm, document what you saw, and know when you are out of your depth.

Start credentials this fall. Clinical enrollment depends on age and course openings.

The semester (~3–4 months)

Book cycle 1 · Medic — One Spark cycle ≈ one semester: ~480 hours over roughly 3–4 months (~16 hrs/wk). Anchor training is the adventure; academics, reading, and reflection run in parallel.
