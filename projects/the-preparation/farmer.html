11th-grade SparkSeason on land

Why this cycle — feed people from land you work

Not a weekend garden. Chores that stack for a season — plus a mentor or host who will sign what he actually did.

  • AgriLife first for local path and livestock referrals.
  • Optional Oahu: WWOOF / Mohala / MAʻO-class hosts with family base.
  • Climax: season log + mentor/host letter.
  • Off the list: buying produce and calling it a harvest.

The call: show up for the chore when you don't feel like it, measure the yield, and thank the host in writing.

Align Oahu travel with school calendar. East TX season still counts if travel slips.
