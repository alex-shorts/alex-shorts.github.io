Why this cycle — read the sky, trust the math

East Texas flight schools are close. The HS climax is the written exam — proof you studied like a professional before the stick time stacks up.

  • Logbook day one. Every discovery flight counts.
  • PHAK is the spine. FAA handbook + structured ground school.
  • Climax: FAA Private Pilot written passed.
  • Later: dual toward solo at 16+.

The call: preflight every time, respect weather, and never rush a checkride you are not ready for.

Book discovery flights early. Weather cancels — stack sessions in fall/winter.
