10th-grade SparkTexas 200

Why this cycle — read the water, crew the boat

Maxim sailed the Falklands; our version is the Texas 200 — dad skipper, son crew. Lake days and ASA 101 come first. The climax is a real passage log, camp notes, and weather journal — not a marina afternoon. Deep-sea charter is the catch-and-eat companion (Chef / Hunter ethics).

  • Lake before Gulf. Knots, points of sail, crew commands on calm water.
  • ASA 101 is the credential gate. Book Kemah / Clear Lake school in winter.
  • Texas 200 in June — register early at texas200.com. Camps each night.
  • Charter week: he baits, helps land fish, IDs species, logs legal keepers.
  • Off the list: substituting a booze cruise, skipping the passage log, or ASA 104 as the HS climax.

The call: learn the knots before you need them, log the wind every day on the water, and crew like the boat depends on you — because it does.

Register Texas 200 early. June slots and camp reservations fill. Dad remains skipper; he is crew with a real logbook.
