Why this combined cycle — bits and atoms

Hacker without Maker is a demo that never leaves the laptop. Maker without Hacker skips the systems that scale. One year ships both.

  • Code track: freeCodeCamp / Godot — public repo from week one.
  • Fab track: Onshape CAD + makerspace prototypes with process photos.
  • Climax: live URL + show/sale proof.
  • Off the list: tutorial-only repos, unfinished half-builds.

The call: ship something ugly this week, photograph the fab step, and ask one real user what broke.

Narrow the product. One app + one physical SKU beats five abandoned ideas.
