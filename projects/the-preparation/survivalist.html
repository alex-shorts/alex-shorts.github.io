Why this cycle — stay calm when the kit is light

Nights out are the adventure. Credentials and practice weekends come first — then NOLS / Outward Bound / TX–NM expedition.

  • WFA before multi-day. No expedition without a current card.
  • Practice weekends with journal and skills video drafts.
  • Climax: multi-day expedition + course cert if offered.
  • Off the list: solo bushcraft stunts without a plan.

The call: pack light, stay warm, treat water, and write what nearly went wrong.

Book expedition early. NOLS / OB slots fill months ahead.
