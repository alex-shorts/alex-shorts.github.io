10th-grade SparkGrading / bout

Why this cycle — take the hit, stay calm, stand up

Fighter is not street fighting. It is months under a coach — pads, bag work, sparring when cleared, and a graded test of composure. Overlaps basketball body work; Psych CLEP rides the class later.

  • Pick a serious gym. Ethos MMA/kickboxing, Bares, or Gracie Barra Tyler — trial first.
  • Nov–Mar block overlaps basketball; schedule around practice, not through it.
  • Climax = grading or amateur bout — coach sets date, sanctioned rules, parent corner.
  • Win not required — finishing the round with control counts.
  • Off the list: backyard boxing, unsupervised sparring, Thailand as substitute for local climax.

The call: show up when you are tired, keep your hands up when you are scared, and let the coach say when you are ready — not Instagram.

Coach sets the climax date. No sparring until cleared. First Aid card before comp route.
