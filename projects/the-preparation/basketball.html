Cycle 22 · 10th-grade Spark — daily craft plus a real coaching assignment. Climax: game film + youth coach eval + season stats. Player and teacher.

10th-grade SparkCoach + film

Why this cycle — play the game, teach the game

Mission-style path: measurables, film, and the ability to run a practice for real players. Coaching makes him rare on a recruiting sheet.

  • Year-round player development — log training, film study, stats.
  • Coaching assignment: youth team, camp assistant, or skills clinic.
  • Climax: game film + signed coaching eval + season stats.
  • TJC kinesiology path when DE-ready.

The call: be the first in the gym and the last to leave — then teach what you drilled.

Line up coaching in fall. Youth seasons need assistants early.

The semester (~3–4 months)

Spark semester · Basketball — One Spark cycle ≈ one semester: ~480 hours over roughly 3–4 months (~16 hrs/wk). Anchor training is the adventure; academics, reading, and reflection run in parallel.
